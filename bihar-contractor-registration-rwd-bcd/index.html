Contractor Registration in Bihar — RWD and BCD Classes, Fees, Validity and the eproc2 Enrolment

Quick answer: Bihar's Rural Works Department and Building Construction Department register contractors department-wise in three classes: Class 1 for tenders of any value at a fee of ₹2,00,000, Class 2 up to ₹3.5 crore at ₹1,00,000, and Class 3 up to ₹70 lakh at ₹25,000 — paid in one instalment, valid five years, renewable at the same fee if applied a month before expiry, and upgradable by paying the difference. RWD applies online at contractor.rwdbihar.gov.in on PAN and Aadhaar authentication for individuals, firms, companies, JVs and a separate unemployed-engineer category; BCD takes the fee by demand draft to the Under Secretary, BCD, Patna. Bidding then needs a separate annual enrolment on eproc2.bihar.gov.in, a Class 3 DSC and a ₹590 processing fee per tender. We file the class application, assemble the solvency and experience proof and set the renewal clock.

Last verified 5 October 2026 — rules on this page checked against the current notifications. We update it the day a rule changes.

Classes, limits and fees (RWD Contractor Registration Rules, as revised; BCD mirrors the same scale)

ClassTender limitRegistration / renewal fee
Class 1Any amount₹2,00,000
Class 2Up to ₹3.5 crore₹1,00,000
Class 3Up to ₹70 lakh₹25,000
  • Fee paid in full in one go (no part payment); validity 5 years; renewal at the same fee, applied for one month before expiry with a one-month grace period in which tendering continues
  • Upgrade to a higher class by paying the difference in fee once the eligibility (turnover, experience, machinery, engineers) is met
  • Each department registers separately — an RWD registration does not qualify you for BCD or RCD tenders
  • Portals: RWD at contractor.rwdbihar.gov.in (individual, proprietorship, partnership, company, unemployed engineers, JV — PAN and Aadhaar authentication, then departmental verification); BCD through the department's online registration form, fee by demand draft payable to the Under Secretary, BCD, at Patna

The fee and limit scale above is as published by the departments and reproduced in their application guidance; the dated rule text (RWD Rules 2007 as amended) is confirmed with the division office before payment. RCD's scale and any 2024–26 amendment are checked at filing — figures circulating online for RCD with a "Class IV" or ₹1 crore limits do not match the departments' published scale.

Then: bidding on eproc2

  • Annual bidder enrolment on eproc2.bihar.gov.in (fee as shown on the portal at enrolment), Class 3 DSC, and a ₹590 (incl. GST) tender processing fee per tender; EMD, performance security and MSE preference per GFR 170/171 and the state rules
  • High-volume departments: RWD, RCD, BCD, PHED, WRD, BREDA, Panchayati Raj — see Bihar tenders

Documents

  • PAN, Aadhaar, GST certificate, cancelled cheque, photo and signature (under 50 KB each)
  • Departmental affidavit (individual / company format); partnership deed or MOA/AOA/COI; degree certificate for unemployed engineers
  • Higher classes: bank solvency certificate, ITRs, work-experience certificates, machinery list, engineering staff; BCD also asks for a character certificate and a no-blacklisting declaration

Frequently asked questions

What is the fee for Class 1 contractor registration in Bihar?

₹2,00,000 for RWD/BCD Class 1, which allows tenders of any value; Class 2 is ₹1,00,000 (up to ₹3.5 crore) and Class 3 ₹25,000 (up to ₹70 lakh).

How long is Bihar contractor registration valid?

Five years; renew at the same fee by applying a month before expiry — a one-month grace period keeps you eligible to tender.

Can I upgrade from Class 3 to Class 2?

Yes — once you meet the higher class's eligibility, by paying the difference in fee.

Is RWD registration valid for BCD or RCD tenders?

No — each department registers contractors separately.

Can an unemployed engineer register?

Yes — RWD has a separate unemployed-engineer category with the degree certificate as the key document.

Is GST mandatory?

Yes — the GST certificate is in the document list for all classes.

What does eproc2 enrolment cost?

An annual enrolment fee shown on the portal at registration, plus ₹590 (incl. GST) per tender as processing fee.

Where is the RWD application filed?

Online at contractor.rwdbihar.gov.in with PAN and Aadhaar authentication, followed by departmental verification.
