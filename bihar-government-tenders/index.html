Bihar Government Tender Consultancy — From Identification to Award

The Government of Bihar is investing heavily in rural infrastructure, e-governance and urban development — and floating tenders to match. We help contractors, IT companies and infrastructure firms find the right Bihar tenders, qualify for them, and win them.

Departments we track

  • Panchayati Raj Department — rural infrastructure projects, community development, village-level procurement and panchayat service contracts.
  • Information Technology Department — e-governance projects, IT infrastructure, software and digital solutions tenders, technology implementation contracts.
  • Urban Housing & Development Department — urban infrastructure, housing development, municipal service contracts and smart-city-linked initiatives.
The buyer map: beyond Panchayati Raj, IT and UDHD, Bihar's steady tender flow comes from BUIDCO (AMRUT 2.0 water & sewerage), Rural Works Department, PHED, Building Construction Department, BMSICL (medical equipment/drug rate contracts) and BELTRON/BSEDC (IT — note this is distinct from BSEIDC, which handles school infrastructure) — all bidding through eProc 2.0 (eproc2.bihar.gov.in), where a Class 3 Digital Signature Certificate is the entry ticket.
Setting up in Bihar, not just bidding? The Bihar Industrial Investment Promotion Package offers a 30% capital subsidy, SGST reimbursement up to 300% of project cost over 14 years, interest subvention up to ₹40 crore, and free land for large investments (₹100 crore+/1,000 jobs) — with the current window running into late 2026. If you're evaluating a Bihar unit alongside tender opportunities, we can guide both tracks together.
The newest high-value verticals for 2026: power discoms SBPDCL/NBPDCL (solar rooftop under PM Surya Ghar, smart meters), BRPNNL (bridges), BSEIDC (school buildings under PM SHRI), JEEViKA/BRLPS (IT hardware, command-centre tech), and Smart City bodies now shifting from capital works to O&M and consultancy contracts. These are genuinely under-bid relative to volume — most consultants still only chase the traditional PWD/RWD tenders.
Scale of opportunity: the Bihar eProcurement portal (eProc 2.0) typically carries 3,600+ active tenders at any given time, spanning roads, bridges, drinking water, power infrastructure, health facilities and urban projects — published daily by departments across all districts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Bihar departments' tenders do you cover?

Our focus areas are the Panchayati Raj Department (rural infrastructure, community development, village-level procurement), the IT Department (e-governance, IT infrastructure, software tenders) and Urban Housing & Development (urban infrastructure, housing projects, municipal contracts, smart city initiatives).

Can a company from outside Bihar bid for Bihar government tenders?

In most cases yes, subject to each tender's eligibility conditions. Where local presence or registration is required, we advise on compliance routes including consortium and JV structures.

Do you help after the tender is won?

Yes — agreements, performance guarantees, milestone documentation and departmental coordination through execution. Winning is half the work; getting paid smoothly is the other half.

How do I know which Bihar tenders my business is eligible for?

Send us your company profile, turnover, experience and certifications — the first consultation includes an honest eligibility screening so you never spend money on bids you cannot win.

What does it cost to register on the Bihar eProcurement portal?

Vendor registration is free, but requires a ₹1,180 non-refundable processing fee to generate your TRN. Your fixed cost is a Class 3 Digital Signature Certificate (with signing and encryption); per-bid costs are the tender fee and EMD, with Udyam-registered MSMEs often getting EMD relaxations.

Does Bihar offer industrial subsidies beyond tenders?

Yes — the Bihar Industrial Investment Promotion Package gives a capital subsidy up to 30%, SGST reimbursement up to 300% of approved project cost for 14 years, interest subvention up to Rs 40 crore, and free or discounted land depending on investment size. It runs alongside tender opportunities for businesses setting up manufacturing in the state.

Can a business outside Bihar bid for Bihar government tenders?

Yes — eProc 2.0 registration isn't restricted to Bihar-based businesses; outside bidders just need the same Class 3 DSC and vendor registration as local ones, though some tenders carry local-preference clauses worth checking case by case.

I'm having trouble logging into the Bihar eProcurement portal — what do I do?

Most login issues trace back to an expired DSC, a browser/Java compatibility problem, or an unmapped digital signature — the eProc 2.0 portal is particular about both. We troubleshoot this directly rather than leaving you stuck mid-bid with a deadline approaching.

What is the eProc2 Bihar registration fee?

A non-refundable ₹1,180 registration fee, payable online via UPI, net banking or card, generates your Temporary Registration Number (TRN). Beyond that, per-tender EMD and document costs apply, and MSME/Udyam-registered bidders often qualify for EMD relaxation.

My DSC or PKI component keeps failing on eProc2 Bihar — how do I fix it?

This is the most common eProc2 problem. You need a valid Class III DSC with both signing and encryption certificates, the portal's PKI/Java component correctly installed, and a compatible browser. Never submit in the final hour before a deadline — PKI errors, slow uploads and payment-gateway delays are exactly what strand last-minute bidders. We set up and test your DSC well before your first submission.
