BIS Hallmark Registration for Jewellers — Free Registration, HUID, Mandatory Districts & Silver (2026)

Quick answer: BIS charges no fee for a jeweller's hallmarking registration or certification — the ₹7,500 / ₹15,000 / ₹40,000 / ₹80,000 turnover slabs still shown on many pages belong to the pre-2021 regime. Registration is online (NSWS / Manakonline); hallmarking itself is done at a BIS-recognised Assaying and Hallmarking Centre which laser-marks the six-character HUID. Mandatory gold hallmarking covered 380 districts from 2 March 2026 and 392 from the August 2026 amendment order; jewellers with turnover up to ₹40 lakh are exempt; silver hallmarking remains voluntary, with HUID compulsory on any silver that is hallmarked since 1 September 2025.

Frequently asked questions

What is the BIS hallmark registration fee for jewellers?

Nil. BIS's own FAQ states no fee is charged for registration or certification; the ₹7,500 slab figures are from before 2021.

Is hallmarking mandatory in Delhi, Noida, Gurugram, Ghaziabad and Patna?

Yes — these districts have been in the mandatory list since the early phases; the list reached 392 districts in August 2026.

Is silver hallmarking mandatory?

No, it is voluntary; but any silver article that is hallmarked must carry HUID under IS 2112:2025 since 1 September 2025.

Who is exempt from mandatory hallmarking?

Jewellers with annual turnover up to ₹40 lakh, plus exports and the other categories listed in the Order.

What is HUID?

A unique six-character alphanumeric code laser-marked on each hallmarked article and recorded in the BIS system.

How many districts are covered now?

392 under the Third Amendment Order of 3 August 2026 (380 from 2 March 2026).

Does the registration expire?

BIS describes jeweller registration as one-time; we confirm any renewal condition on your certificate at filing.

What is the penalty for selling un-hallmarked gold in a mandatory district?

An offence under the BIS Act 2016 — penalty up to five times the value of the article or imprisonment, besides seizure.
