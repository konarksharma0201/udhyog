BIS QCO List 2026 — What Was Withdrawn, What Is Enforced, What Still Needs ISI

Quick answer: A Quality Control Order makes BIS certification mandatory for a product. In October–November 2025 the Centre withdrew 21+ QCOs on raw materials — polypropylene, polyethylene, PVC, ABS, polycarbonate, PTA, MEG, polyester yarns and fibres, fatty acids, and aluminium, copper, nickel, tin and zinc — while enforcing orders on electrical equipment (10 Nov 2025), furniture (Feb 2026) and household electrical appliances (19 Mar 2026). Finished goods keep their own QCOs. We check the live BIS list for your HS code before you certify or stop certifying.

These followed the August 2025 regulatory-reform committee. Its wider recommendation — 27 QCOs scrapped and 112 products suspended, including footwear and electronic components — is not fully notified; treat footwear, electronics and finished-goods QCOs as in force unless a gazette says otherwise.

How to read your position in three steps: (1) find the IS number for your product on the BIS "products under compulsory certification" list; (2) check whether the QCO that imposed it is still in force — the November 2025 withdrawals are listed above; (3) note the scheme, because fees and timelines differ. Raw-material relief does not release the finished product: PVC resin is out, PVC pipes and cables are not.

Frequently asked questions

Which QCOs were withdrawn in November 2025?

Fourteen chemical and polymer orders on 12 November 2025 (PP, PE, PVC, ABS, polycarbonate, PTA, MEG, polyester yarns/fibres), the Ministry of Mines' metals orders on 13 November (aluminium, copper, nickel, tin, zinc), six fatty acids on 22 October and six more chemicals on 20 November.

Is BIS still required for polyester yarn, PVC or polypropylene imports?

Not under the rescinded QCOs — but products made from them (pipes, cables, appliances, furniture) keep their own orders where notified.

Were the footwear and electronic-component QCOs withdrawn?

Not as of 4 October 2026. The committee recommended it; no gazette has withdrawn them. Treat them as in force.

Which electrical products became mandatory in 2025-26?

Electrical equipment under Scheme-X from 10 November 2025; household, commercial and similar electrical appliances from 19 March 2026; furniture from February 2026.

How do I check if my product needs BIS certification?

Match your product to an Indian Standard on the BIS compulsory-certification list, confirm the imposing QCO is still live, and identify the scheme (ISI, CRS or Scheme-X). We do this against the live list, not a cached blog table.

What does BIS certification cost?

CRS ₹28,000 plus tax with one test report; ISI ₹1,000 application, ₹1,000 a year licence, ₹7,000 per inspection man-day plus marking fee (80% off for micro/startup, 50% for small enterprises); Scheme-X ₹2,000 application and ₹25,000 a year. Testing is extra.
