Delhi Government Offices for Business Compliance — Verified Addresses & What They Handle

Quick answer: A Delhi compliance file goes to a specific office: ROC Delhi & Haryana at IFCI Tower, Nehru Place; the EPFO zonal office in Wazirpur and regional offices by area; ESIC's regional office at Rajendra Place with sub-regional offices in Okhla, Rohini and Nand Nagri; the Trade Marks Registry at the IP Office, Dwarka; DGFT's CLA; the Excise Department; and DPCC. We keep this list verified against the official directories because most guides copy outdated addresses.

Last verified 4 October 2026 — rules on this page checked against the current notifications. We update it the day a rule changes.

How to use this page

  • Match your establishment's address to the regional office before filing — a file lodged at the wrong EPFO or ESIC office is returned, not forwarded
  • For hearings (GST, trademark, excise) note that the issuing office, not the nearest one, is where you appear
  • Patna equivalents are on the Patna employer registrations page (EPFO RO R-Block, ESIC RO J.L. Nehru Marg) and the Bihar practice page

Frequently asked questions

Where is the ROC for Delhi companies?

Registrar of Companies, NCT of Delhi & Haryana — 4th Floor, IFCI Tower, 61 Nehru Place, New Delhi 110019 (roc.delhi@mca.gov.in); the MCA contact page also lists ROC Delhi II at Lok Nayak Bhawan, Khan Market, so confirm which registry holds your file.

Where is the ESIC regional office in Delhi?

Rajendra Bhawan, DDA Complex, Rajendra Place, New Delhi 110008 (011-25745044), with sub-regional offices at Okhla, Rohini and Nand Nagri.

Which EPFO office covers my Delhi establishment?

The regional office for your area — East (Jhilmil), North and Central (Wazirpur), West (Dwarka) — under the Delhi & Uttarakhand zonal office at Wazirpur. Listings differ, so confirm with the office before a visit.

Is there an EPFO office at Bhikaji Cama Place?

The building houses EPFO vigilance and internal-audit functions; we found no official listing of a regional office for employers there.

Where are trademark hearings for Delhi held?

At the Trade Marks Registry, Delhi — IP Office, Boudhik Sampada Bhawan, Sector 14, Dwarka — increasingly by video.

Which office handles a Delhi liquor licence?

The Department of Excise, Entertainment & Luxury Tax, GNCTD (Vikas Bhawan, IP Estate), for L-17 and other hotel, club and restaurant licences.
