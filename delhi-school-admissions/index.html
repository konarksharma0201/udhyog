Delhi Nursery & School Admission Guidance — DoE 2026-27 Cycle

Quick answer: For 2026-27, a child must be at least 3 years old as on 31 March 2026 for Nursery (a one-month relaxation is possible at the Head of School's discretion). Application forms open 4 December 2025; schools publish their points criteria by 28 November 2025. Registration is capped at a non-refundable Rs 25 fee — capitation fees and compulsory prospectus purchases are prohibited by the Directorate of Education.

What we verified directly from the DoE circular (most guidance sites blur this): the Directorate of Education's own notification caps the nursery registration fee at Rs 25, non-refundable — no school in Delhi may lawfully charge more for registration, demand a capitation fee, or make prospectus purchase compulsory. If a school's process suggests otherwise, that's a compliance red flag worth raising, not a norm to accept.

The points system — what actually decides admission

This is where we spend most of our time with a family — reading your target schools' published points criteria against your actual address, sibling and alumni status, and telling you honestly where you stand before you apply.

Each school designs its own points criteria within DoE rules, typically weighted across neighbourhood/distance from the school, sibling already enrolled, alumni (parent is a former student), and sometimes single-parent or girl-child points. Schools must publish their full points break-up on their website before the form goes live, and any draw of lots for tied scores must happen in the presence of parents, recorded on video. Reading a school's published criteria carefully — before applying, not after — is the single highest-leverage thing a parent can do.

Documents you'll typically need

  • Address proof (ration/smart card, recent utility bill, or passport) in a parent's or the child's name
  • Child's birth certificate
  • Recent photographs of the child and both parents
  • Quota-specific documents where applicable: sibling's ID/fee receipt for sibling points, a religious-minority certificate, or EWS/DG category documents

Every school may ask for a slightly different exact document set — we confirm the specific list against your target schools' current notices before you submit anything.

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum age for Nursery admission in Delhi for 2026-27?

A child must be at least 3 years old as on 31 March 2026, per the Directorate of Education's circular for the 2026-27 session. Schools may grant up to one month of age relaxation at the Head of School's discretion.

How much can a Delhi school charge for a nursery registration form?

The Directorate of Education caps the registration fee at Rs 25, non-refundable. Capitation fees are prohibited, and schools cannot make purchasing their prospectus compulsory for registration.

What is the points system in Delhi nursery admissions?

Each private unaided school sets its own points criteria within DoE rules — commonly covering neighbourhood distance, sibling enrollment, alumni status and sometimes other factors — and must publish the full break-up on its website before the application window opens. Where scores tie, a draw of lots must be conducted in front of parents and recorded on video.

When do Delhi nursery admission forms open for 2026-27?

Per the DoE's circular for this cycle, application forms became available from 4 December 2025, with schools required to publish their criteria and points system by 28 November 2025 and the form-submission deadline set at 27 December 2025. Always check the current notice, as dates can be revised.

Can a nursery admission consultant guarantee my child a seat?

No legitimate consultant can guarantee a seat in Delhi's DoE-regulated points-based system — admission is decided by the published criteria and, where scores tie, a recorded draw of lots. What an honest consultant offers is help reading criteria correctly, preparing a complete and accurate application, and not missing deadlines — not a guaranteed outcome.

Are EWS/DG admissions on the same timeline as general admissions?

No — EWS, DG (Disadvantaged Group) and CWSN admissions run on a separate track managed directly by the DoE, with their own schedule, distinct from the open-category dates and points process described here.
