Frequently asked questions

Which are the hardest schools to get into in Delhi?

Among Delhi's most consistently competitive schools by reputation, ranking and reported demand are Delhi Public School R.K. Puram, The Shri Ram School (Vasant Vihar), Vasant Valley School, Sanskriti School, The Mother's International School, Sardar Patel Vidyalaya, Modern School (Barakhamba Road), Springdales School (Pusa Road), St. Columba's School, and Delhi Public School (Mathura Road).

Which are the most expensive schools in Delhi?

By reported and official fee schedules, The British School (Chanakyapuri) and the American Embassy School — both international-curriculum schools — sit at the top of Delhi's fee scale, followed by CBSE schools such as Vasant Valley and Modern School Barakhamba Road.

Is admission to these schools decided by the school's management?

No — for CBSE/CISCE day schools, entry-level (Nursery/KG/Class 1) admission in Delhi runs on the Directorate of Education's points system (neighbourhood distance, sibling, alumni points) with a videographed draw of lots for ties. International schools like The British School and American Embassy School use their own published priority-category and application-date processes.

Can a consultancy guarantee my child a seat at one of these schools?

No legitimate consultancy can. The Delhi DoE's own circular warns against anyone claiming to influence or guarantee admission and prohibits schools from charging any consultancy or facilitation fee tied to an outcome. Reputable help is limited to documentation, points-criteria interpretation and deadline tracking.

Are fee figures on this page official or estimated?

Both, and each is clearly labelled. Only The British School and the American Embassy School publish detailed official fee schedules; for most CBSE/CISCE day schools on this list, published figures are third-party aggregator estimates rather than school-confirmed numbers — always verify directly with the school.
