DIN, DSC and Director KYC (DIR-3 KYC) — The 31 March 2026 Change, Due Dates, Documents and DIN Reactivation

Quick answer: A Director Identification Number is allotted once and stays with the person for life; it is applied for in SPICe+ for a new company's directors or in Form DIR-3 for an existing company, with PAN, Aadhaar, address proof and a photograph. A Digital Signature Certificate (Class 3) signs every MCA form and is sold in one-, two- and three-year validities. The Companies (Appointment and Qualification of Directors) Amendment Rules, 2025 (G.S.R. 943(E) of 31 December 2025, in force from 31 March 2026) replaced the annual DIR-3 KYC with a filing once every three consecutive financial years, due by 30 June of the year following the third year; a missed filing deactivates the DIN until DIR-3 KYC is filed with a ₹5,000 fee. Many pages still say 30 September every year — that is the old rule.

Frequently asked questions

What is a DIN?

A Director Identification Number — a unique number allotted by the MCA to a person who is or wants to be a director; it is issued once and remains valid for life.

What documents are needed for DIN?

PAN, Aadhaar, proof of address such as a bank statement or utility bill, and a passport-size photograph; foreign nationals use a passport with apostille or consular attestation.

What is the due date for DIR-3 KYC now?

Once every three consecutive financial years, by 30 June of the year following the third financial year, under the rule in force from 31 March 2026.

What is the penalty for missing DIR-3 KYC?

The DIN is deactivated and can be reactivated by filing DIR-3 KYC with a ₹5,000 fee.

Does changing my mobile number reset the three-year KYC cycle?

No — the change is reported separately within 30 days with a ₹500 fee, and the three-year due date stays where it was.

What is a DSC and which class do I need?

A Digital Signature Certificate that signs electronic filings; a Class 3 DSC is used for MCA, GST, income-tax and tender portals.

What is the DSC full form?

Digital Signature Certificate.

Is there an annual DIN renewal?

No — a DIN has no renewal; only the periodic KYC keeps it active.
