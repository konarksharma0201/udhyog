Does DPS Mathura Road offer a sibling or alumni quota?

Yes, the school reserves a specific percentage of seats for siblings of current students and children of alumni. This requires precise documentation of the sibling's current enrollment or the parent's alumni status.

Can I apply to multiple branches of DPS Delhi?

DPS Society guidelines generally restrict parents to applying to only one branch within the Delhi region to ensure fair distribution of seats. Applying to multiple branches may lead to the cancellation of all applications.
