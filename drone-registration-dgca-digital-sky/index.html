Drone Registration & Licensing in India — UIN, Remote Pilot Certificate & Type Certificate

Quick answer: Every drone above 250 g must be registered on DGCA's Digital Sky platform for a Unique Identification Number (Form D-2, ₹100 via Bharatkosh). Nano drones need nothing; micro drones (250 g–2 kg) need a UIN and a Remote Pilot Certificate only for commercial use; small drones (2–25 kg) and above need UIN, an RPC from a DGCA-authorised RPTO (₹100, valid 10 years) and a type-certified model. We handle the UIN, pilot certification route and type-certificate questions for survey, agriculture, logistics and media operators in Delhi-NCR and Bihar.

The three approvals

  • UIN — Form D-2 on Digital Sky with the drone's serial number and (for non-nano) the manufacturer's type certificate; fee ₹100 per drone through Bharatkosh; UIN displayed on the airframe
  • Remote Pilot Certificate — since the February 2022 amendment the DGCA licence was replaced by an RPC issued through DGCA-authorised Remote Pilot Training Organisations; ₹100 issuance fee, valid 10 years, generated on Digital Sky after the RPTO uploads results (training itself is paid to the RPTO)
  • Type certificate — Form D-1, model-level certification under the QCI-run scheme; not required for nano and model (R&D) drones; Digital Sky links your UIN to the manufacturer's TC, so an uncertified model cannot be registered
Airspace and what is changing: Digital Sky's map divides airspace into green (fly without permission within height limits), yellow (ATC permission) and red (no-fly without central clearance) zones — the whole of central Delhi and airport surrounds are red/yellow, so NCR operators plan by the map, not by habit. The Ministry published a draft Civil Drone (Promotion and Regulation) Bill in September 2025 for consultation; until enacted, the Drone Rules 2021 as amended remain in force.

Frequently asked questions

What is the fee for drone registration in India?

₹100 per drone for the Unique Identification Number on Digital Sky (Form D-2), paid through Bharatkosh; the Remote Pilot Certificate issuance fee is also ₹100.

Do I need to register a drone under 250 grams?

No — nano drones are exempt from UIN and from pilot certification, subject to the height and zone limits.

Do I need a Remote Pilot Certificate for a micro drone?

Only for commercial operations; recreational flying of a 250 g–2 kg drone needs a UIN but no RPC. Small drones (above 2 kg) always need one.

How is the Remote Pilot Certificate obtained?

Through a DGCA-authorised Remote Pilot Training Organisation — training and assessment at the RPTO, then the certificate is issued on Digital Sky (₹100), valid 10 years.

What is a drone type certificate?

A DGCA certification of the drone model under the QCI scheme (Form D-1); required for all categories above nano and for commercially sold drones — your UIN application is linked to it.

Can I fly a drone in Delhi?

Only where Digital Sky's map shows a green or (with ATC permission) yellow zone; central Delhi, airport approaches and sensitive sites are red. Check the live map before every flight.

Are the rules changing?

A draft Civil Drone (Promotion and Regulation) Bill was released for consultation in September 2025; the Drone Rules 2021 (as amended in 2022) remain the operative law until it is enacted.

What do agriculture spraying drones need?

A type-certified drone with a UIN, a pilot holding an RPC from an RPTO, and compliance with CIB&RC spraying SOPs; subsidy schemes layer on top of these.
