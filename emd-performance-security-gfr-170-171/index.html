EMD and Performance Security in Government Tenders — What GFR 170 and 171 Actually Say in 2026

Quick answer: Under the General Financial Rules 2017, bid security (EMD) is 2–5% of the estimated value — or a bid-securing declaration in its place — under Rule 170, and Udyam-registered MSEs and DPIIT-recognised startups are exempt. Performance security under Rule 171 was reduced by the Finance Ministry's OM of 29 December 2023 to 3–5% of contract value for goods, consultancy and non-consultancy services; only works carry 3–10% including retention. Most tender guides still quote 5–10%. Layered on top are the MSE procurement policy (25% earmark, L1+15% matching) and the Make in India order (Class-I 50% / Class-II 20% local content). We read the bid document against these rules before you lock money in.

Why it matters on a ₹2 crore supply contract: performance security at 5% is ₹10 lakh locked for the contract plus warranty; at the old 10% it was ₹20 lakh. A bid document still demanding 10% on goods is out of line with the 2023 OM and can be challenged in the pre-bid query. MSEs should also check that EMD exemption is honoured and not replaced by a "₹0 bid security declaration with higher PBG".

Frequently asked questions

What is the performance security percentage under GFR 171?

3–5% of contract value for goods, consultancy and non-consultancy services after the Finance Ministry's OM of 29 December 2023; works remain at 3–10% including retention.

How much EMD is required under GFR 170?

2–5% of the estimated value, or a bid-securing declaration in place of money; Udyam MSEs and DPIIT-recognised startups are exempt.

Is 10% performance security still valid for a goods tender?

It is out of line with the 2023 OM for central procurement; raise it as a pre-bid deviation. State rules may differ.

What does L1 + 15% mean for an MSE?

An MSE whose quote is within 15% of the lowest bid may match the L1 price and take at least 25% of the quantity under the PPP-MSE Order.

What is a Class-I local supplier?

A supplier with at least 50% local content under the Make in India order; Class-II is 20–50%; only Class-I gets purchase preference.

Is a bid-securing declaration safer than EMD?

It costs nothing up front, but a breach (withdrawal, non-signing) leads to debarment for the period stated in the tender.

Which DSC is needed to bid?

A Class 3 digital signature certificate for CPPP, state e-procurement portals and GeM.

Does GeM also follow GFR 171?

Yes — GeM contracts take performance security per GFR 171; buying modes follow GFR 149 (₹50,000 / ₹10 lakh thresholds).
