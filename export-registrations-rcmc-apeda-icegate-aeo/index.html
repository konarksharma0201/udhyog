Export Registrations in India — RCMC, APEDA, ICEGATE, AEO, Spices Board and AGMARK: Order, Documents, Validity

Quick answer: A new exporter needs, in this order: PAN and GST, the Import Export Code from DGFT, an AD code registered with the bank at each port, an ICEGATE login on the customs portal (valid IEC and a Class 3 digital signature are mandatory), and an RCMC from the export promotion council or commodity board for the product. The RCMC is valid for five financial years, from 1 April of the year it is issued to 31 March of the fifth year, and its fee is fixed by each council, not by DGFT. Exporters of scheduled agricultural and processed-food products register with APEDA, and spice exporters with the Spices Board. Frequent traders can add AEO status: tiers T1 and T2 are valid three years and T3 five years, and T3 can only follow two years as a T2 holder.

RCMC — who issues it, what it costs, how long it lasts

  • What it is: Registration-cum-Membership Certificate — proof that you are a registered exporter of a product line with the council or board named for it in the Foreign Trade Policy; it is needed to claim benefits under the policy
  • Which body: by main product — APEDA for agricultural and processed foods, MPEDA for marine products, and councils such as engineering, apparel, gems and jewellery, pharmaceuticals, chemicals and services; if your goods fall under several, you declare the main line of business
  • Validity: deemed valid from 1 April of the licensing year in which it is issued and runs for five financial years ending 31 March, unless the body specifies otherwise (DGFT e-RCMC page)
  • Fee: set by each council and by turnover band; there is no single government rate, so we quote it from the council's current schedule
  • Application: online through DGFT's e-RCMC system to the council or board, with IEC, GST certificate, PAN, bank certificate and, for service exporters, export data certified by a chartered accountant

APEDA, Spices Board and AGMARK

  • APEDA: exporters of the scheduled agricultural and processed-food products register with APEDA and hold its RCMC; this is a legal requirement under the APEDA Act for those products, not only a way to claim benefits
  • Spices Board: exporters of spices register with the Spices Board under its governing Act
  • AGMARK: the certification mark for agricultural commodities graded under the Agricultural Produce (Grading and Marking) Act; it is voluntary unless a statute or order makes it compulsory for a specific commodity, and it is a separate system from FSSAI — see FSSAI

Frequently asked questions

What is RCMC?

A Registration-cum-Membership Certificate issued by the export promotion council, commodity board or development authority for your product, needed to claim benefits under the Foreign Trade Policy.

How long is an RCMC valid?

Five financial years — from 1 April of the licensing year it is issued, ending 31 March of the fifth year, unless the issuing body specifies otherwise.

What are the RCMC fees?

Each council or board fixes its own membership fee, often by turnover band; there is no single government rate, so check the council's current schedule.

Is APEDA registration mandatory?

Yes for exporters of the scheduled agricultural and processed-food products under the APEDA Act; they register with APEDA and hold its RCMC.

How do I apply for APEDA registration?

Through the DGFT e-RCMC system to APEDA with IEC, GST certificate, PAN, bank certificate and the product details.

How do I register on ICEGATE?

Open icegate.gov.in, choose the Importer/Exporter role, verify GSTIN and IEC, upload documents and sign with a Class 3 DSC; the ID and password are emailed after CBIC approval.

What is AEO certification and what does it cost?

A CBIC trusted-trader status in tiers T1, T2 and T3 (plus LO for logistics operators); CBIC charges no government fee, and the effort lies in documentation and the site audit.

Is AGMARK mandatory?

Generally voluntary, except where a statute or order makes it compulsory for a particular commodity; it is separate from FSSAI registration.
