Fire NOC in Delhi, Gurugram/Faridabad & Noida — Thresholds, Validity and the Law That Actually Applies

Quick answer: In Delhi a fire NOC under the Delhi Fire Service Rules 2010 (Rule 27) and UBBL 2016 is needed for residential buildings above 15 m (G+4), hotels and guest houses above 12 m, educational, institutional and mercantile buildings above 9 m (G+2), every assembly building, business buildings above 15 m, storage above 250 sq m and hazardous occupancies — and, per the DFS notice of June 2026, buildings with two or more basement levels. The fire safety certificate runs 5 years for residential and 3 for non-residential. In Haryana the governing law is the Fire and Emergency Services Act 2022 (the 2009 Act is repealed); the ULB portal states no fee and a 60-day timeline for scheme approval. We handle the building-plan NOC, the fire safety certificate and renewals.

Three myths on ranking pages: that Haryana still works under the 2009 Act (repealed by the 2022 Act); that Haryana NOCs cost ₹2,000–₹50,000 (the ULB portal says nil); and that every NOC is renewed yearly (Delhi is 5/3 years; Haryana specified buildings 3 years).

Documents

  • Sanctioned building plans and occupancy; CLU where applicable
  • Fire-fighting scheme — detection, hydrants, sprinklers, pumps, staircases and refuge per NBC Part 4
  • Structural stability certificate; electrical safety; lift and DG details
  • Completion certificate and consultant's compliance report at the certificate stage

Frequently asked questions

Which buildings need a fire NOC in Delhi?

Residential above 15 m, hotels/guest houses above 12 m, educational/institutional/mercantile above 9 m, all assembly buildings, business above 15 m, storage above 250 sq m, hazardous occupancies, and buildings with two or more basement levels (June 2026 notice).

Does a restaurant need a fire NOC?

If it is assembly occupancy, yes regardless of height; we check the seating and the building's classification before filing.

How long is a Delhi fire NOC valid?

The fire safety certificate runs 5 years for residential and 3 years for non-residential buildings including hotels.

What is the fire NOC fee in Gurugram?

The Haryana ULB portal states no fee for approval or renewal; the RTS timeline for scheme approval is 60 days.

Is the Haryana Fire Services Act 2009 still in force?

No — the Haryana Fire and Emergency Services Act 2022 repealed it.

Who issues the fire NOC in Faridabad?

The Commissioner, MC Faridabad, on the recommendation of the Fire Officer, through the ULB portal.

Does a two-level basement need a fire NOC in Delhi?

Yes — added by the Delhi Fire Service notice of June 2026.

How do I apply in Noida?

Through Nivesh Mitra to UP Fire and Emergency Services; the fee and timeline are shown on the portal at filing.
