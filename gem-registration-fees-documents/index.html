GeM Fees, Documents & Timelines — The Page Consultants Don't Publish

The most common question about GeM is also the one most consultant websites dodge: "what will this actually cost me, and what exactly do I need?" Here are the straight answers — government charges, refundable deposits, our role, and a checklist you can act on today.

What the government charges (and doesn't)

  • Seller registration: free. Anyone charging you a "GeM government registration fee" is mislabelling their service fee.
  • Caution money: refundable, turnover-based. A security deposit by slab — smaller sellers pay less — held in your GeM wallet and refundable per policy. Budget it as parked capital, not spent money.
  • Vendor assessment: category-dependent. Required mainly for OEMs and certain quality-marked categories; carries an official agency fee, with policy exemptions for specific seller classes. Many authorised resellers skip it entirely — verify before paying.
  • Transaction charge: a small percentage on order values above thresholds, per GeM's current policy — a cost of doing business that healthy pricing absorbs.

The complete documents checklist

  1. PAN (proprietor / firm / company as applicable)
  2. GST registration certificate
  3. Bank account details with cancelled cheque
  4. Udyam (MSME) certificate — unlocks EMD exemptions and purchase preference
  5. Authorised person's Aadhaar with linked mobile (OTP verification)
  6. Business address proof
  7. For brand/OEM routes: trademark certificate or brand authorisation letter
  8. ITR details for turnover-based features, where sought

Realistic timelines

Registration: 3–5 working days with the checklist complete. Catalogue live: same week if specifications and images are ready. Vendor assessment (where needed): additional weeks depending on the agency's schedule — start early if your category demands it. First bid participation: often within the first fortnight.

Our fee philosophy: quoted upfront in the first consultation, in writing, before any work begins — separated clearly from government-side amounts so you always know which rupee goes where. Judge every consultant you talk to by the same standard.

Frequently asked questions

Is GeM seller registration really free?

Yes — GeM charges nothing for seller registration itself. Costs arise later: caution money (a refundable security by turnover slab), vendor assessment fees where applicable, and a small transaction charge on order value above thresholds. Consultant fees, including ours, are for professional setup and management — never a government registration fee.

What is caution money on GeM?

A one-time refundable security deposit required before certain selling activities, set by seller turnover slab — lower for smaller sellers. It sits as a wallet balance, is adjustable against dues, and is refundable per GeM policy; it is parked capital, not a fee you lose.

Is vendor assessment mandatory and what does it cost?

It is required mainly for OEMs and certain quality-marked categories; resellers with OEM authorisation often do not need it. It carries an official fee payable to GeM's designated assessing agency, with exemptions for specific seller classes per current policy — we confirm your exact requirement before you pay anything.

What documents should I keep ready?

PAN, GST registration, bank account with cancelled cheque, Udyam certificate for MSME benefits, the authorised person's Aadhaar linked with mobile, business address proof, and for OEM/brand routes a trademark or brand-authorisation document. With this set complete, registration typically finishes in 3-5 working days.
