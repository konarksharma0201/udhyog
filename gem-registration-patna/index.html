Frequently Asked Questions

Can a Patna business sell to departments outside Bihar on GeM?

Yes — GeM is a national marketplace. A Patna-registered seller can receive orders from any central or state buyer across India; your catalogue quality and pricing decide your reach.

What is needed for Bihar eProc 2.0 registration?

A Class 3 Digital Signature Certificate, a registered user ID on eproc2.bihar.gov.in, and your firm documents (PAN, GST, Udyam). We handle DSC procurement, portal registration and bid submission end to end.

Do Bihar tenders give MSME benefits?

Registered MSMEs commonly get EMD exemptions and reduced tender-document fees on uploading a valid Udyam certificate — check each NIT's conditions. We ensure your Udyam and GeM profiles are set up to claim these benefits.

How fast can my GeM account be ready?

With PAN, GST, bank details and Udyam in hand, seller registration typically completes in 3–5 working days; catalogue upload and brand/OEM steps follow immediately after.
