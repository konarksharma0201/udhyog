GeM Registration & Government Tender Bidding Consultancy

Quick answer: GeM seller registration is free and takes 3–5 working days: gem.gov.in → Sign Up → Seller → Aadhaar/PAN OTP → GST, bank (penny-drop) and Udyam details → catalogue. Manufacturers add Vendor Assessment (₹11,200 + GST via the designated agency) for OEM status. Caution money is no longer required (discontinued for all sellers since August 2025).

The Government e-Marketplace (GeM) is where central and state departments, PSUs and institutions now buy goods and services — over 1.6 lakh government buyers and 22.5 lakh+ sellers, with GMV of ₹4.09 lakh crore in just the first ten months of FY24-25 and cumulative GMV past ₹18 lakh crore. If your business is not registered, catalogued and bid-ready on GeM, you are invisible to India's largest buyer. We fix that — registration to award.

Frequently asked questions

What documents are required for GeM registration?

GST registration, PAN, bank account details, Udyam (MSME) registration, address proof, and the authorised person's Aadhaar/PAN. With complete documents, seller registration typically completes in 3–5 working days.

What is vendor assessment on GeM and is it mandatory?

Vendor assessment validates a seller's manufacturing/service capability on the GeM portal. It is required for OEMs in many categories and unlocks higher-value participation. We manage the assessment process end to end.

What is the OEM panel on GeM?

Original Equipment Manufacturers can register on the OEM panel to control their catalogue and authorise resellers/distributors. It protects your brand on the portal and is often required for brand-approval categories.

Can you bid on tenders on our behalf?

Yes — end to end: identifying relevant GeM bids and e-procurement tenders, preparing technical and financial bids, participating in RAs, and handling post-award compliance. You focus on delivery; we handle the paperwork.

What happens if my GeM registration application is rejected?

We review the rejection reason (usually a document or bank-verification mismatch), correct it, and refile — most rejections are fixable within days, not a fresh start.

Can I register on GeM as an individual or sole proprietor, without a company?

Yes — proprietorships, partnerships, LLPs, companies and individuals can all register as sellers, provided PAN, GST (where applicable) and bank details are in order.

Are there products or services that can't be sold on GeM?

Yes — some categories are restricted or require prior approvals (certain regulated goods, specific licensed services); we confirm your category's status before you invest in catalogue-building.

What if I forget my GeM User ID or password?

Use the 'Forgot User ID' or 'Forgot Password' links on the GeM login page, verified via your registered mobile or email OTP. If the linked mobile number has also changed, recovery needs a formal request through GeM support — we handle this for clients so an old login doesn't block active bids.

Does being a Delhi-NCR seller actually help on GeM?

For products, GeM is location-neutral. For services and on-site contracts it genuinely helps: the densest concentration of government buyer offices in India is in Delhi-NCR, and bids requiring local delivery or deployment favour sellers who can serve Delhi, Noida, Gurugram, Ghaziabad and Faridabad directly. We position NCR sellers' catalogues and bids to capture that local demand.

What is GeM vendor assessment, and do I need it?

Vendor assessment is GeM's verification of a seller's manufacturing capability — it's what unlocks OEM status, letting you list your own products under your own brand rather than reselling. The assessment is conducted online against your registration documents, premises and production evidence, and stays valid for a defined period before renewal. If you manufacture anything, OEM status is worth pursuing: it protects your catalogue from resellers. We prepare the documentation and manage the assessment end to end.

Is my MSME exempt from EMD in government tenders?

Registered MSEs (with Udyam/NSIC) and DPIIT-recognised startups are generally exempt from Earnest Money Deposit on GeM and government tenders — but the exemption applies to the items/services covered by your registration, and each tender's terms govern. Traders reselling goods they don't manufacture may not qualify. We check the exemption against the specific NIT before you assume it.

Can two companies bid together as a JV or consortium?

Yes, where the tender permits it — typically the partners must collectively meet 100% of the financial pre-qualification, with the lead member meeting a higher individual share and each member a minimum, backed by a consortium agreement and a power of attorney to the lead. We structure the JV so eligibility criteria are actually satisfied on paper the way evaluators read them.

What is a Performance Bank Guarantee (PBG), and what does it cost?

After winning, you furnish a PBG — commonly in the 3%–10% range of contract value depending on the tender — which the bank issues against margin/collateral at a commission of roughly 0.5%–2% per year. That carrying cost belongs in your bid price from the start; we build it into the financial bid rather than letting it surprise you post-award.
