Frequently asked questions

What documents are required for GeM registration?

GST registration, PAN, bank account details, Udyam (MSME) registration, address proof, and the authorised person's Aadhaar/PAN. With complete documents, seller registration typically completes in 3–5 working days.

What is vendor assessment on GeM and is it mandatory?

Vendor assessment validates a seller's manufacturing/service capability on the GeM portal. It is required for OEMs in many categories and unlocks higher-value participation. We manage the assessment process end to end.

What is the OEM panel on GeM?

Original Equipment Manufacturers can register on the OEM panel to control their catalogue and authorise resellers/distributors. It protects your brand on the portal and is often required for brand-approval categories.

Can you bid on tenders on our behalf?

Yes — end to end: identifying relevant GeM bids and e-procurement tenders, preparing technical and financial bids, participating in RAs, and handling post-award compliance. You focus on delivery; we handle the paperwork.
