GS1 Barcode (GTIN) Registration in India — What It Actually Costs and Who Can Apply

Quick answer: Barcodes for retail and marketplace listings come from GS1 India, the not-for-profit set up with the Commerce Ministry — not from resellers. Fees depend on turnover, number of barcodes and subscription period: under the table effective 1 October 2025, a company with turnover up to ₹5 crore taking 100 barcodes for one year paid ₹26,000 registration + ₹12,250 annual subscription + ₹6,885 GST + ₹3,000 refundable security deposit = ₹48,135. GS1 India has published a revised table effective 1 October 2026 — we quote from it at filing. Only the brand owner may apply; processing is 7–10 working days.

Intermediate slabs, and options for 1,000 / 10,000 / 1,00,000 barcodes and 2–10 year subscriptions, are in the GS1 India schedule. A revised schedule applies from 1 October 2026; figures are confirmed against it at filing. Pages quoting "₹3,000–₹22,000" or an "₹11,800 deposit" are wrong.

Documents and process

  • PAN of the entity
  • Turnover proof for the latest financial year (not needed for a new company)
  • Request letter on letterhead
  • Status proof — GST / partnership deed / incorporation certificate
  • Cancelled cheque

Apply on gs1india.org; GTINs are allotted in DataKart; allotment in 7–10 working days. Only the brand owner (or an entity it authorises) can hold the company prefix — job-workers and resellers cannot.

Frequently asked questions

How much does barcode registration cost in India?

Under GS1 India's October 2025 table, ₹48,135 in year one for turnover up to ₹5 crore and 100 barcodes (₹26,000 + ₹12,250 + GST + ₹3,000 refundable deposit); a revised table applies from 1 October 2026.

Is a GS1 barcode mandatory for Amazon or Flipkart?

Marketplaces generally require GS1-issued GTINs (or a brand exemption) for product listings.

How long does GS1 registration take?

7–10 working days after documents and payment.

Is the security deposit refundable?

Yes — ₹3,000, interest-free, refundable on exit.

Do I need to renew?

Yes, by the subscription period chosen (1 to 10 years).

Can a job-worker or trader register barcodes for a brand?

No — only the brand owner or an entity it authorises.

Can a new company apply without turnover proof?

Yes; turnover proof is waived for newly formed companies.

Are cheap barcodes from resellers valid?

They are not licensed to you by GS1 and can be rejected by marketplaces and retailers.
