Frequently asked questions

Which GST authorities handle Noida businesses?

Noida and Greater Noida taxpayers fall under Uttar Pradesh state GST jurisdiction (Gautam Buddha Nagar) or central jurisdiction depending on allocation — the notice itself tells you which. We represent before both.

Can you handle both my Delhi and Noida GSTINs?

Yes — multi-GSTIN businesses across Delhi-NCR are our core clientele. One team handles notices, audits and reconciliations across your registrations so positions stay consistent.

What are the most common GST notices Noida businesses receive?

ITC mismatch intimations (DRC-01C), scrutiny notices (ASMT-10) and show cause notices (DRC-01) built on GSTR-1 vs 3B or 2B vs 3B differences — most defensible with proper reconciliation if answered in time.

Do you visit our office for audit preparation?

For audit and assessment matters, yes — document review and preparation at your Noida/Greater Noida premises can be arranged so the audit file is hearing-ready.

How much does GST registration or filing cost in Noida?

Registration and routine filing are commodity services with modest market rates; disputes are priced by stakes and complexity. We give a written fixed quote after a free first look — and we're candid when a matter is simple enough to not need us.

Can you represent me if I can't be physically present?

Yes — for most hearings and departmental visits we attend on your behalf with a proper authorisation (Vakalatnama or letter of authority); you don't need to travel unless the matter specifically requires your personal presence.

Can I register for GST in Noida using a virtual office?

Yes — this is common for e-commerce sellers needing a UP address. You'll need a compliant virtual-office agreement, NOC and utility bill that match exactly, since Noida's CGST Commissionerate verifies new registrations closely. We prepare verification-ready documentation to avoid a REG-03 query or rejection.
