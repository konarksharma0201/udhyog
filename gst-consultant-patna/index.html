Frequently asked questions

Where are GST appeals for Patna orders filed?

First appeals against orders of Deputy/Assistant Commissioners go to the Joint Commissioner (Appeals) under Section 107 with a 10% pre-deposit. Second appeals now lie before the GSTAT State Bench for Bihar, and writ matters before the Patna High Court.

Do you appear in person before Patna GST authorities?

Yes — hearings, audit proceedings and follow-ups are attended physically. For Bihar matters, presence before the officer is often the difference between a file that moves and a file that sits.

My GST registration or bank account has been frozen — can you help?

Yes. Provisional attachment and registration suspension matters need fast, structured responses — representation before the jurisdictional officer, and appellate or writ remedies where the action is disproportionate.

Do you take small routine GST filings in Patna?

Our Patna practice focuses on substantial matters — significant demands, audits, investigations and appeals. For routine monthly filing, a local accountant is usually more economical; for disputes with real money at stake, that is exactly what we do.

How much does a GST consultant charge in Patna?

For routine work like registration or monthly filing, Patna market rates are modest; for notice replies, audits and appeals, fees depend on the demand amount and complexity. We quote a fixed, written fee after a free first review of your notice or requirement — before any work begins.

Which areas of Patna do you serve?

All of Patna and its business belts — Boring Road, Fraser Road, Kankarbagh, Ashiana-Digha, Patliputra, Danapur, Phulwari Sharif and beyond — plus matters from every Bihar district, since assessment records and we both travel.
