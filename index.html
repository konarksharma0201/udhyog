How much subsidy can I get under the PMFME scheme?

We prepare and facilitate your 35% PMFME credit-linked capital subsidy application up to ₹10 lakh for individual micro food processing units. SHG members can also get ₹40,000 seed capital per member, and group projects by SHGs, FPOs and cooperatives can access larger credit-linked grants. Full details on our PMFME subsidy page.

How do I reply to a GST DRC-01 show cause notice?

A DRC-01 reply must be precise, complete, and on time. Our team drafts your Form DRC-06 reply on the GST portal, normally within 30 days, addressing every ground point-by-point with reconciliations and case law. Step-by-step process on our GST notice reply page — and if the notice just arrived, start with what to do in the first 48 hours.

What happens if I ignore a GST notice?

The officer can confirm the demand through an order in DRC-07 with tax, interest and penalty, and recovery can begin. Responding within the deadline is the only way to contest or reduce a demand — call us the day the notice arrives, not the week the deadline ends.

How long does GeM registration take?

With complete documents — GST, PAN, bank details and Udyam registration — GeM seller registration typically completes in 3 to 5 working days. See the full checklist on our GeM & tender page.

Why do applications move faster with Udyog Growth?

Because we work with the same departments every week. We prepare every file in the exact format each office expects, submit it to the right desk the first time, and follow up in person until it reaches a decision. Most delays and rejections come from avoidable process errors — that's precisely the gap we close.

Which MSME schemes can my business benefit from?

Depending on your business: Udyam Registration, PMEGP subsidy for new units, CGTMSE collateral-free credit guarantee, ZED Certification, Lean Manufacturing scheme and MSE-CDP cluster support. Eligibility check is free — details on our MSME schemes page.
