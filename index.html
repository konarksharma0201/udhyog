Questions people actually search

How much subsidy can I get under the PMFME scheme?

PMFME gives a 35% credit-linked capital subsidy up to ₹10 lakh for individual micro food processing units. SHG members can also get ₹40,000 seed capital per member, and group projects by SHGs, FPOs and cooperatives can access larger credit-linked grants. We prepare your DPR, align your project with your district's ODOP, and coordinate with the bank and state nodal agency until sanction.

How do I reply to a GST DRC-01 show cause notice?

A DRC-01 must be answered in Form DRC-06 on the GST portal, normally within 30 days. The reply should address every ground point-by-point with reconciliations — GSTR-1 vs GSTR-3B for turnover gaps, GSTR-2B vs GSTR-3B for ITC mismatch — plus supporting case law. We draft the complete legal reply, compile annexures and represent you in the personal hearing.

What happens if I ignore a GST notice?

The officer can confirm the demand through an order in DRC-07 with tax, interest and penalty, and recovery can begin. Responding within the deadline is the only way to contest or reduce a demand — call us the day the notice arrives, not the week the deadline ends.

How long does GeM registration take?

With complete documents — GST, PAN, bank details and Udyam registration — GeM seller registration typically completes in 3 to 5 working days. We handle registration, catalogue upload, vendor assessment and OEM panel so you can start bidding quickly.

Which MSME schemes can my business benefit from?

Depending on your business: Udyam Registration, PMEGP subsidy for new units, CGTMSE collateral-free credit guarantee, ZED Certification, Lean Manufacturing scheme and MSE-CDP cluster support. We assess eligibility free in the first consultation.
