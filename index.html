Frequently asked questions

What does Udyog Growth do?

Udyog Growth is an Indian consultancy that helps businesses obtain government approvals, claim MoFPI and MSME subsidies, defend GST litigation and audits, and win government tenders including GeM. We act as a single liaisoning partner between your business and government departments.

Which government subsidy schemes do you handle?

MoFPI schemes such as PMKSY, PMFME, PLI, Mega Food Parks and Cold Chain subsidies, and MSME Ministry schemes such as PMEGP, CGTMSE, ZED Certification, Udyam Registration and MSE-CDP.

Do you handle GST notices and litigation?

Yes. We draft replies to Show Cause Notices, represent clients in GST audits, assessments, ITC disputes and DGGI investigations, and handle appeals before Appellate Authorities, GST Tribunals and High Courts.
