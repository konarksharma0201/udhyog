NGO Registration in Patna — Trust, Society or Section 8, Then 12A/80G

Quick answer: In Patna an NGO is formed as a public trust (deed registered at the Sub-Registrar), a society (Registrar of Societies, Bihar, under the 1860 Act) or a Section 8 company (ROC Patna via SPICe+), then takes 12A/80G from CIT (Exemptions) Patna — now Forms 104/105 under the Income-tax Act 2025 — NGO Darpan, CSR-1 and, when eligible, FCRA. We choose the structure for your funding plan and run the sequence.

After formation — the sequence funders check: PAN → 12A and 80G (CIT Exemptions, Patna; Forms 104 provisional / 105 regular under the 2025 Act) → NGO Darpan (NITI Aayog) → CSR-1 on MCA for corporate CSR → FCRA registration or prior permission when foreign funding is in view (3-year / ₹15 lakh test; 2026 Rules apply). Bihar-specific: Professional Tax registration if you employ staff above the slab, and Shop Act registration for the office.

Frequently asked questions

Should I register a trust or a Section 8 company in Patna?

Section 8 if you expect CSR or institutional grants, foreign funding or professional governance; a trust if the NGO is founder- or family-led with a defined endowment; a society for membership-based community bodies. Funders increasingly prefer Section 8.

Where is a trust registered in Patna?

At the Sub-Registrar office for the area where the trust's office is, on a stamped trust deed signed by the settlor and trustees with witnesses and ID proof.

How many members does a society need in Bihar?

Seven, with a memorandum and rules filed with the Registrar of Societies (IG Registration, Bihar) under the Societies Registration Act, 1860.

Which office handles 12A/80G for a Patna NGO?

The Commissioner of Income Tax (Exemptions), Patna — applications are online in Form 104 (provisional) and Form 105 (regular) under the Income-tax Act, 2025.

Is NGO Darpan registration mandatory?

Required to receive government grants and increasingly asked for by CSR donors; it is free and links your PAN to a unique ID on the NITI Aayog portal.

Does a Patna NGO need professional tax and Shop Act registration?

If it employs staff — yes to Bihar professional tax above the slab, and Shop Act registration for the office; EPF at 20+ and ESIC at 10+ employees.
