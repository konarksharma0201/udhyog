Privacy Notice — How Udyog Growth Uses the Details You Share

Quick answer: When you tap Call or WhatsApp on this site we ask for your name, mobile number and email so we can follow up on your enquiry. We use them only to respond to you and to keep a record of the enquiry, we keep them in our Google Workspace tools (Gmail and Google Sheets), we do not sell them, and you can ask us to correct or delete them at any time at udyoggrowth@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Details you type: name, mobile number and email address in the form that opens before a call or WhatsApp chat
  • About the click: whether you chose call or WhatsApp, the page you were on, the pre-filled WhatsApp message, and the time
  • Technical and campaign data: device type (mobile or desktop), the site or advertisement that sent you (referrer and campaign tags) and the Google Analytics client identifier

Why we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry by call, WhatsApp or email and to follow up on it
  • To keep a record of enquiries, see which pages generate them and improve the site
  • We do not use your details for unrelated marketing, and we do not sell them

Where it is stored and who sees it

  • Your details reach our lead sheet in Google Sheets and a notification email in our Gmail account, both operated by Google under the account udyoggrowth@gmail.com; only the team handling enquiries has access
  • Site visits are measured with Google Analytics (GA4), which uses cookies and similar identifiers
  • Google acts as our service provider for these tools and may process data on servers outside India

Your browser remembers you for 30 days

After you submit the form we save your name, mobile number and email in your own browser for 30 days, so that you are not asked again on every page. Clearing the site data in your browser removes it. Returning visitors' call and WhatsApp clicks are logged against the same details.

Your rights and how to reach us

  • You can ask us what we hold about you, correct it, withdraw your consent, or have it erased — write to udyoggrowth@gmail.com or call us
  • We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond and follow up, and delete them when you ask or when they are no longer needed
  • This site is for businesses and adults; it is not meant for anyone under 18
  • Complaints about how we handle your data can be sent to the same email and phone; if the matter is not resolved you may approach the Data Protection Board of India under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 — see our DPDP guide for businesses

We update this notice when our practice changes; the version on this page is the current one.
