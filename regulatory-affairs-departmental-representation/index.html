Regulatory Affairs & Departmental Representation

Quick answer: Regulatory affairs support covers the documentation, correspondence, follow-up and representation work that sits between a business and a government department — hearing preparation, show-cause response, filing follow-up, inspection coordination and multi-department coordination — across any scheme or authority, in Delhi-NCR and Bihar.

Last verified 4 October 2026 — rules on this page checked against the current notifications. We update it the day a rule changes.

What regulatory affairs support actually covers

Regulatory affairs is the connective work between a business and a government department — the documentation, correspondence, follow-up and representation that turns a scheme or a licensing rule into an actual approval in hand. It cuts across every scheme-specific service we offer (PMFME, FSSAI, GST, GeM, CLU and the rest) rather than replacing any of them; this page covers the generic, process-level services that apply whichever department you're dealing with.

Central & state government affairs advisory

Central-government schemes and state-government schemes run on different departments, different portals and often different documentation — we track which authority actually owns a given approval before filing, so a Delhi-NCR or Bihar client isn't sent to the wrong desk.

Institutional & public sector representation

Beyond individual scheme filings, we handle broader institutional engagement — stakeholder mapping for a multi-department project, public authority communication, and project facilitation where a client needs a single point of coordination across several approvals rather than managing each one separately.

Who this is for

Businesses, manufacturers, NGOs, cooperatives and institutions anywhere in Delhi-NCR or Bihar dealing with any central or state government department — whether that's a single pending application, a stuck file that needs follow-up, a show-cause notice that needs a response, or a project that needs coordination across multiple approvals at once.

Frequently asked questions

What does a regulatory affairs consultant actually do?

A regulatory affairs consultant prepares and files your applications to departmental standards, tracks the file through the government system, responds to queries and show-cause notices on your behalf, represents you at hearings and inspections, and coordinates between departments where an approval touches more than one authority. The work is procedural and representational — we do not have the power to approve anything ourselves.

What is the difference between regulatory affairs support and a government approval itself?

Regulatory affairs support is the professional service of preparing your file correctly and representing you before the department; the approval itself is issued only by the government authority, on merit, through its own process. No consultancy — including ours — can guarantee an outcome; what we guarantee is that your file is complete, correctly addressed, and followed up consistently.

How do you handle a government show-cause notice or departmental query?

We read the notice or query in full, identify exactly what the department is asking for, assemble the supporting documents, draft a point-by-point written response, and represent you at the hearing if one is scheduled. Response deadlines in government matters are often short and non-negotiable, so we prioritise these the day they arrive.

Can you coordinate an approval that involves more than one government department?

Yes — many projects (an industrial unit needing land conversion, pollution consent and a factory licence together, for example) require sequential or parallel approvals across departments. We map the full sequence up front and coordinate it as one file rather than leaving you to manage each department separately.

How do you track the status of a pending government application?

We maintain our own tracker for every client file, check the relevant portal or department directly rather than relying only on the applicant's own follow-up, and flag any query or objection the moment it's raised so it can be answered inside the deadline.

Do you handle renewal and post-approval compliance, or only the first application?

Both. A license or approval that lapses because a renewal deadline was missed is one of the most common and entirely avoidable problems businesses face — we track renewal dates and ongoing compliance reporting for existing clients, not just the first-time filing.
