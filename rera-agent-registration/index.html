RERA Agent Registration — Haryana (Gurugram), UP (Noida), Delhi & Bihar in 2026

Quick answer: No one may facilitate the sale of a RERA-registered project without agent registration under Section 9 — and registration is state-wise. HARERA Gurugram charges ₹50,000 for an individual or proprietorship and ₹2,50,000 for any other entity, plus a ₹5,000 scrutiny fee; UP RERA now admits applications only after a mandatory four-day training and exam (₹6,000), and existing UP agents must be certified by 31 December 2026. Registration runs five years. A Delhi registration does not cover Noida or Gurugram.

Last verified 4 October 2026 — rules on this page checked against the current notifications. We update it the day a rule changes.

Two things ranking pages miss: Haryana's individual fee doubled to ₹50,000 (Cabinet, March 2024) — most still show ₹25,000; and UP RERA's office order of 5 January 2026 makes a four-day non-residential training plus exam a precondition for registration, renewal and amendment, with the digital certificate uploaded on the Agent Dashboard before the form even opens — existing agents have until 31 December 2026.

Which authority

  • Gurugram district projects: HARERA Gurugram; other Haryana districts incl. Faridabad: HRERA Panchkula (jurisdiction confirmed per project at filing)
  • Noida / Greater Noida / Ghaziabad: UP RERA (NCR office in Greater Noida)
  • Delhi: Delhi RERA; Patna: RERA Bihar
  • Selling across NCR means two or three registrations — one per state

Documents and process

  • PAN, Aadhaar/address proof, photograph
  • ITRs for three years or a declaration
  • Entity documents — partnership deed / MoA-AoA / LLP agreement; registered office proof
  • Haryana: Form REA-I (certificate in REA-II); UP: training certificate first
  • Validity five years; Haryana renewal applied at least three months before expiry (Form REA-IV)

Frequently asked questions

What is the RERA agent registration fee in Gurugram?

₹50,000 for an individual or proprietorship and ₹2,50,000 for other entities, plus a ₹5,000 scrutiny fee (HARERA Gurugram).

Is training mandatory for UP RERA agents?

Yes — a four-day training and exam (₹6,000 per participant) under UP RERA's order of 5 January 2026; the certificate must be uploaded before the registration form opens.

What is the deadline for existing UP agents?

31 December 2026 to obtain the training certificate.

How long is agent registration valid?

Five years; in Haryana apply for renewal at least three months before expiry (Form REA-IV).

Can a Delhi RERA agent sell Noida or Gurugram projects?

No — each state needs its own registration (UP RERA / HARERA).

What is the fee in Delhi and Bihar?

₹10,000 for individuals and ₹50,000 for other entities on current published schedules; we confirm on the authority portal at filing.

What documents are needed?

PAN, address proof, photograph, three years' ITRs or a declaration, and entity documents; UP adds the training certificate.

What is the penalty for operating without registration?

Section 62: up to ₹10,000 a day of default, which may extend to 5% of the cost of the unit sold.
