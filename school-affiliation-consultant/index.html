School Affiliation & Recognition Consultant — CBSE, CISCE, DoE Recognition (New Delhi)

Quick answer: 2025-26 rule change most consultants haven't caught up with: CBSE's State NOC is now optional — apply with or without it, and if the state stays silent for 30 days (plus a 15-day reminder), a deemed-NOC applies. But the State Recognition Certificate is still mandatory — no NOC does not mean no state clearance. The Rs 5 lakh "security deposit" myth: widely repeated online, but there is no such mandatory deposit for a senior secondary school. The Rs 5,00,000 figure in CBSE's fee schedule is the society-to-society transfer fee — a completely different head. Official fresh-affiliation fees are far lower: Rs 1,50,000 up to Secondary, Rs 2,50,000 up to Senior Secondary, plus Rs 5,000 annual. We work from the current fee schedule, not recycled blog numbers.

CBSE affiliation — eligibility, land, fees

Only a registered Society, Public Trust or Section 8 company can hold CBSE affiliation — never a proprietary or for-profit company. Affiliation is staged: fresh affiliation up to Secondary first (initial term 3 years), Senior Secondary comes by upgradation after running Classes 9-10, then 5-year extension cycles.

SARAS 7.0 — the current application cycle

CBSE now runs affiliation through the SARAS portal (currently version 7.0). Two pre-conditions before you can even file: a UDISE+ code and a completed SQAAF self-assessment. Fresh affiliation and upgradation applications now attract a mandatory 100% physical inspection — there is no longer a documents-only fast track. We track every SARAS window and portal update so a client's file is ready the day it opens, not scrambling after a deadline extension.

CISCE / ICSE affiliation

The Council for the Indian School Certificate Examinations affiliates schools to a similarly non-profit entity (Society/Trust/Section 8 company), with comparable land, staffing and PTR expectations, a qualified principal (5+ years' experience), and a properly constituted managing committee. CISCE affiliation hinges heavily on the inspection report — we prepare the file and the premises together, not sequentially.

Delhi Directorate of Education recognition

The DoE has scrapped the old "essentiality certificate" requirement and moved to a self-certification model — a school certifies against a checklist of infrastructure, safety, teacher and PTR standards (73 checkpoints, down from 91) rather than waiting on a discretionary essentiality clearance. Recognition under the Delhi School Education Act & Rules, 1973 remains mandatory regardless of CBSE's own optional-NOC change — the two are separate documents, and a school still needs the State Recognition Certificate before its CBSE/CISCE file can go through.

Since November 2025, the DoE has also moved to recognise private unaided schools operating in previously non-conforming areas — bringing an estimated ~500 additional schools under DoE oversight and creating roughly 20,000 extra EWS/DG/CWSN seats citywide. If your school (or a site you're evaluating) sits in an area that was earlier treated as non-conforming, this is worth re-checking before assuming recognition is blocked.

RTE compliance & the 25% EWS quota

Private unaided non-minority schools must reserve 25% of entry-level seats for EWS/DG students under Section 12(1)(c) of the RTE Act, with state fee reimbursement under Section 12(2). Delhi runs a computerised EWS lottery (the 2025-26 draw allotted around 42,000 seats). A Supreme Court bench, by order dated 13 January 2026, directed that securing this quota be treated as a national mission and impleaded the NCPCR to monitor compliance — enforcement is tightening, not easing. We help schools build the compliant admission and reimbursement-claim process from the outset rather than fixing it under audit pressure.

UDISE+ registration

The 11-digit UDISE+ code is filed via Form A02 through your Block/District MIS office — not a self-serve online form — and requires state recognition first. Realistic timelines run 4-6 weeks in metros to 8-12 weeks in rural areas; the July-September window gets congested. Since UDISE+ is itself a SARAS pre-condition, a delay here delays affiliation.

Fire & building safety NOC for schools

An educational building in Delhi taller than 9 m or with ground plus 2 upper storeys needs a fire safety certificate under the Delhi Fire Service Act 2007 and NBC 2016 Part 4 — a two-stage process (pre-construction plan approval, then post-construction certification), typically valid 3 years, filed through the Delhi Fire Service. A separate building-safety/structural-stability certificate from the local body or a licensed architect is required for CBSE regardless of the fire NOC.

School canteen FSSAI, entity formation & educational land

Three approvals in this journey are already deep specialities of ours on other pages — we run them as one file for a school client rather than three separate engagements: FSSAI registration/license for the canteen or hostel mess (HFSS-food restrictions apply within and near school premises), Society/Trust/Section 8 company formation for the school's managing body, and change of land use (CLU) for educational purposes in Delhi if the site isn't already zoned for institutional use.

School bus & transport compliance

CBSE-mandated norms (implementing Supreme Court guidelines under the Motor Vehicles Act 1988): yellow paint with "School Bus"/"On School Duty" signage, a speed governor capped at 40 km/h, GPS and CCTV, grilled windows with mesh, a female attendant, a trained driver with a clean record, a designated Transport Manager, and a valid RTO permit from the state transport department.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is CBSE State NOC still mandatory for a new school in 2026?

No — from the 2026-27 session, CBSE's State NOC is optional. A school can apply with or without it; if without, CBSE refers the matter to the state, and a deemed-NOC applies if the state stays silent for 30 days plus a 15-day reminder. This does not remove the separate, still-mandatory State Recognition Certificate under the RTE Act — the two are different documents.

How much land do I need to open a CBSE school?

It depends on land category: 1,600-2,400 sq m in Category C cities (Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata, Bengaluru, Hyderabad and a few other areas), 2,400-3,200 sq m in Category B, and 6,000 sq m in Category A elsewhere. Note land area now only sets your category — the number of sections you can actually run is governed separately by built-up area, at 3 sections per 400 sq m.

How much does CBSE affiliation actually cost?

Official fees: Rs 1,50,000 for fresh affiliation up to Secondary, Rs 2,50,000 up to Senior Secondary, Rs 50,000 for the Middle Class Syllabus (Classes 1-5), and Rs 5,000 annual. There is no mandatory Rs 5 lakh 'security deposit' for a senior secondary school — that figure is the separate society-to-society transfer fee, often misquoted online.

Can a private company set up and run a CBSE school?

No — CBSE only affiliates schools run by a registered Society, Public Trust or Section 8 (not-for-profit) company. A proprietary or for-profit private limited company cannot hold CBSE affiliation, so the first step for a new school is choosing and registering the right non-profit entity.

What is SARAS 7.0 and what do I need before applying?

SARAS is CBSE's online affiliation portal, currently on version 7.0. Two pre-conditions before filing: a UDISE+ code and a completed SQAAF self-assessment. Fresh affiliation and upgradation applications now require a mandatory 100% physical inspection — there's no documents-only route anymore.

Do I need a UDISE+ code before applying for CBSE affiliation?

Yes — UDISE+ is a precondition for filing on SARAS. It's an 11-digit code obtained via Form A02 through your Block/District MIS office (not a self-serve online form), and it requires state recognition to already be in place. Budget 4-6 weeks in metros to 8-12 weeks in rural areas, and avoid applying in the congested July-September window.

What fire safety rules apply to a school building in Delhi?

A school building taller than 9 m, or with ground plus 2 upper storeys, needs a Delhi Fire Service certificate under the Delhi Fire Service Act 2007 and NBC 2016 — a two-stage process covering pre-construction plan approval and post-construction certification, typically valid 3 years. This is separate from, and in addition to, the building-safety/structural-stability certificate CBSE requires.

Is the 25% EWS quota compulsory for private schools, and is enforcement getting stricter?

Yes — Section 12(1)(c) of the RTE Act requires private unaided non-minority schools to reserve 25% of entry-level seats for EWS/DG students, with state fee reimbursement. A Supreme Court bench, by order dated 13 January 2026, directed that this be treated as a national mission and brought in the NCPCR to monitor compliance — schools should expect tighter enforcement, not a rollback.

My school's land was earlier in a "non-conforming area" — can it still be recognised?

Possibly, and more easily than before. Since November 2025 the DoE has moved to recognise private unaided schools even in previously non-conforming areas, bringing an estimated 500 additional schools under its oversight. Zoning and land-use still need to be checked and, if required, regularised — but the blanket bar that existed earlier has been relaxed.

Do I need a separate FSSAI license for the school canteen?

Yes — canteens and hostel mess facilities need FSSAI Basic Registration or a State License depending on turnover, and FSSAI's charter separately restricts high-fat/sugar/salt (HFSS) food within and near school premises. We run this alongside the affiliation file rather than as an afterthought — see our dedicated FSSAI page for the full slab and process detail.

What happens if my CBSE affiliation extension lapses?

A lapsed extension puts the school's ability to conduct board exams and issue valid certificates at risk. Extension fees are tiered by how late you apply (Rs 50,000-1,50,000) plus a late fee of Rs 25,000/month capped at Rs 1,00,000 — so the cost of delay compounds quickly. We track extension timelines for clients well before the 5-year cycle turns over.
