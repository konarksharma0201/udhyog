The British School, Chanakyapuri — Admission & Fees Guide

Quick answer: The British School (Dr. Jose P. Rizal Marg, Chanakyapuri) is an IGCSE and IB school founded in 1963, with ~60 nationalities on a 5-star GRIHA-rated green campus. Its 2025-26 official fee schedule lists a non-refundable Rs 30,000 registration fee and total-at-admission payments of Rs 10.06-13.61 lakh depending on year group.

Independent information page. Udyog Growth is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an agent of this school. Why admission here is exclusive

An international curriculum, small class sizes, an ~60-nationality community and among the highest fees of any Delhi school make The British School primarily accessible to international/expatriate and affluent Indian families, admitted through defined priority categories.

Frequently asked questions

How much are British School Delhi fees?

Per the school's own 2025-26 schedule: a non-refundable registration fee of Rs 30,000, total payable at admission of Rs 10.06-13.61 lakh (by year group), quarterly term fees of Rs 2.16-3.46 lakh, and a one-time development fee of Rs 6.25 lakh (75% subsidised for Years 12-13).

Which curriculum does The British School follow?

IGCSE (Cambridge International) in secondary years, and the IB Diploma Programme and Career-related Programme in senior years, with EYFS/the English National Curriculum in the primary years.

What is the registration fee at The British School?

Rs 30,000, non-refundable, and payment does not guarantee a place — per the school's official 2025-26 fee schedule.

Is The British School only for expats?

No, but its community includes roughly 60 nationalities and it primarily serves international/expatriate and affluent Indian families given its curriculum and fee structure.

Where is The British School located?

Dr. Jose P. Rizal Marg, Chanakyapuri, New Delhi, on a 5-star GRIHA-rated green campus.
