Delhi Public School, Mathura Road — Admission & Fees Guide

Quick answer: Delhi Public School, Mathura Road (Sundar Nagar) is the flagship and oldest school of the DPS Society, founded in 1949, with around 6,500 students and a day-cum-boarding format, motto 'Service before Self'.

Independent information page. Udyog Growth is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an agent of this school. Frequently asked questions

How old is DPS Mathura Road?

Founded in 1949, it is the oldest and flagship school of the Delhi Public School Society.

What is the fee of DPS Mathura Road?

Aggregator sources cite an initial demand draft (covering registration, admission and first-quarter tuition) of around Rs 75,000 — not a school-confirmed current figure; contact the school directly.

Does DPS Mathura Road have boarding?

Yes, it operates as a day-cum-boarding school.

Which board is DPS Mathura Road?

CBSE.

How to apply to DPS Mathura Road nursery?

DPS Mathura Road follows Delhi's standard DoE nursery points-and-lottery process, like every other private unaided school in the city.
