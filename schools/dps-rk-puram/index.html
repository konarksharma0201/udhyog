Delhi Public School, R.K. Puram — Admission, Fees & Cutoff Guide

Quick answer: DPS R.K. Puram (Sector XII) is one of Delhi's most sought-after CBSE schools, founded in 1972. Its junior wings run through DPS Vasant Vihar and DPS East of Kailash, both of which post competitive shortlist-to-waitlist ratios each cycle. Admission follows the DoE's points-and-lottery system, not a management-decided process.

Why admission here is considered difficult

DPS R.K. Puram's own junior wings are run through sister campuses DPS Vasant Vihar (216 nursery seats) and DPS East of Kailash (180 nursery seats) — both feed into R.K. Puram for later classes, and both are highly oversubscribed. At DPS East of Kailash, the 2025-26 cycle shortlisted 108 applicants with 245 on the waitlist; the 2026-27 first list shortlisted 90 with 150 waitlisted (source: Daily Pioneer, 23 January 2026). Selection runs on the DoE's 100-point neighbourhood/sibling/alumni criteria, with a videographed draw of lots for tied scores — not a discretionary process.

Frequently asked questions

How many nursery seats does DPS R.K. Puram have?

DPS R.K. Puram's junior intake runs through its sister campuses — DPS Vasant Vihar (216 nursery seats) and DPS East of Kailash (180 nursery seats) — which is where R.K. Puram's own nursery-level demand is absorbed. Contact the school directly for the current cycle's confirmed seat count.

What is the admission fee at DPS R.K. Puram?

For the 2024-25 cycle, the school's own published notice asked shortlisted parents for a demand draft of Rs 95,000, against approximate total annual fees of around Rs 2,35,000 excluding transport (official, DPS R.K. Puram). The DoE-wide registration fee is capped at Rs 25. Always confirm the current cycle's figure with the school.

Is DPS R.K. Puram hard to get into?

Yes, by reputation and by the oversubscription visible at its sister campuses — DPS East of Kailash's 2026-27 first list shortlisted 90 applicants against 150 on the waitlist. Selection follows the DoE's points-and-lottery system rather than any discretionary process.

Does DPS R.K. Puram conduct a draw of lots?

Where applicants tie on DoE points, private unaided schools including DPS R.K. Puram's network are required to break the tie via a draw of lots conducted in front of parents and recorded on video, per Delhi DoE rules.

Where do DPS R.K. Puram's youngest students study?

Nursery and early primary intake for the DPS R.K. Puram network runs through the junior wings at DPS Vasant Vihar and DPS East of Kailash, which is standard practice for large DPS Society campuses in Delhi.
