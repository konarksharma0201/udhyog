Modern School, Barakhamba Road — Admission & Fees Guide

Quick answer: Modern School, Barakhamba Road is a CBSE day-cum-boarding school founded in 1920, ranked #1 Vintage Legacy Day-cum-Boarding School by the EducationWorld India School Rankings 2025-26. It is run by the Modern School Society, which also runs sister campuses at Vasant Vihar, Faridabad and Kundli.

Independent information page. Udyog Growth is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an agent of this school. We do not guarantee admission and, in line with the Delhi Directorate of Education's March 2025 circular, we do not charge any donation, capitation or “facilitation” fee tied to an admission outcome. Fee figures below are dated and sourced as shown — always verify current fees and criteria directly with the school and with the DoE at edudel.nic.in.

Why admission here is considered difficult

Century-old prestige, a central Mandi House location and the EducationWorld India School Rankings 2025-26's #1 Vintage Legacy Day-cum-Boarding placement sustain very high demand against a large but still limited intake. Boarding is available for both boys and girls.

Frequently asked questions

How old is Modern School Barakhamba Road?
