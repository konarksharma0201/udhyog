The Mother's International School — Admission & Fees Guide

Quick answer: The Mother's International School (Vijay Mandal Enclave, Kalu Sarai) is a CBSE school founded in 1956, run by the Sri Aurobindo Education Society and located on the Sri Aurobindo Ashram campus, following an integral-education philosophy.

Independent information page. Udyog Growth is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an agent of this school. We do not guarantee admission and, in line with the Delhi Directorate of Education's March 2025 circular, we do not charge any donation, capitation or “facilitation” fee tied to an admission outcome. Fee figures below are dated and sourced as shown — always verify current fees and criteria directly with the school and with the DoE at edudel.nic.in.

Why admission here is considered difficult

Frequently asked questions

Is Mother's International School a CBSE school?

Yes, The Mother's International School follows the CBSE curriculum.

Who runs Mother's International School?

It is run by the Sri Aurobindo Education Society and is located on the Sri Aurobindo Ashram campus in Kalu Sarai.

What is the fee of Mother's International School?

Aggregator estimates place nursery-level monthly tuition around Rs 13,000-15,700, with first-year totals around Rs 95,600-1,88,400 — these are not school-confirmed figures; verify current fees with the school directly.

How to apply to MIS nursery?

The Mother's International School follows Delhi's standard DoE nursery points-and-lottery process, like every other private unaided school in the city.

Where is Mother's International School located?

Vijay Mandal Enclave, Kalu Sarai, on Sri Aurobindo Marg in South Delhi.
