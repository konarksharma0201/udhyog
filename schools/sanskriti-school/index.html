Sanskriti School, Chanakyapuri — Admission & Fees Guide

Frequently asked questions

Who can apply to Sanskriti School under the priority category?

Sanskriti School was originally established primarily for children of All India & Allied Services and Defence personnel — a distinctive, publicly reported priority category. The school is now open to all applicants under standard DoE norms, alongside 25% EWS reservation in place since 2004.

Is Sanskriti School only for civil servants' children?

No — while it was set up primarily for All India & Allied Services and Defence families, admission is now open to all applicants through the standard DoE process, with the historical priority category still a distinctive feature.

What is the fee of Sanskriti School?

Aggregator estimates place Class 1 monthly tuition around Rs 13,165, but published total-cost figures vary and are internally inconsistent across sources — confirm current fees directly with the school.

Which board is Sanskriti School affiliated to?

CBSE.

How many students study at Sanskriti School?

Approximately 2,500-2,859 students, on a campus of about 7.7 acres.
