Sardar Patel Vidyalaya, Lodhi Estate — Admission & Fees Guide

Quick answer: Sardar Patel Vidyalaya (Lodhi Estate) is a CBSE school founded in 1958 by the Gujarat Education Society, known for experiential pedagogy with Hindi-medium roots in the primary years, on a 4.7-acre campus with a roughly 15:1 student-teacher ratio.

Independent information page. Udyog Growth is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an agent of this school. We do not guarantee admission and, in line with the Delhi Directorate of Education's March 2025 circular, we do not charge any donation, capitation or “facilitation” fee tied to an admission outcome. Fee figures below are dated and sourced as shown — always verify current fees and criteria directly with the school and with the DoE at edudel.nic.in.

Why admission here is considered difficult

SPV's progressive, experiential teaching philosophy has built a strong following among Delhi parents over six decades, while its compact campus keeps intake naturally limited. No official applicant-to-seat ratio is published.

Frequently asked questions

Is Sardar Patel Vidyalaya a Hindi-medium school?

SPV has Hindi-medium roots in its primary years as part of its distinctive experiential pedagogy, while following the CBSE curriculum overall.

What is the fee of Sardar Patel Vidyalaya?

Aggregator estimates place nursery monthly tuition around Rs 11,608 and first-year totals around Rs 1.22-1.39 lakh — these are not school-confirmed figures; verify with the school directly.

Which board is SPV affiliated to?

CBSE.

Does SPV have a hostel?

No — Sardar Patel Vidyalaya is a day school.

How to apply to SPV nursery?

SPV follows Delhi's standard DoE nursery points-and-lottery process, like every other private unaided school in the city.
