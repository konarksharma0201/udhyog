The Shri Ram School, Vasant Vihar — Admission & Fees Guide

Quick answer: The Shri Ram School's junior campus at Vasant Vihar (Nursery to Class V) was ranked India's #1 Day Co-ed School by the EducationWorld India School Rankings 2025-26 — a jump from #7 the previous year. Students move to the senior campus at Moulsari, Gurugram, from Class VI. The school follows the ICSE board in Delhi and DoE nursery admission norms.

Frequently asked questions

Which board does The Shri Ram School follow?

The Vasant Vihar junior campus (Nursery to Class V) follows the ICSE board. Students then move to the Moulsari campus in Gurugram, which follows IB and IGCSE curricula for senior classes.

Is The Shri Ram School only up to Class 5 in Delhi?

Yes — the Vasant Vihar campus runs Nursery through Class V. Senior schooling continues at the Moulsari, Gurugram campus.

How is The Shri Ram School ranked?

The EducationWorld India School Rankings 2025-26 placed it as India's #1 Day Co-ed School, up from #7 the previous year, based on a nationwide parent/expert survey covering 34 cities.

What are The Shri Ram School's fees?

The school does not publicly disclose its fee structure; figures circulating on aggregator sites are estimates, not school-confirmed data. Contact the school's admissions office directly for the current cycle's fees.

How do I apply for nursery admission at The Shri Ram School?

Admission (branded 'Pravesh Vatika') follows Delhi's DoE nursery guidelines like every other private unaided school in the city — apply through the school's official channels within the DoE's published window, and read the school's own published points criteria carefully before applying.
