St. Columba's School, Gole Market — Admission & Fees Guide

Quick answer: St. Columba's School (Ashok Place, Gole Market) is a CBSE boys' school founded in 1941, run by the Congregation of Christian Brothers, with around 3,400-3,500 students in the heart of Lutyens' Delhi and a long-reported waiting list.

Independent information page. Udyog Growth is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an agent of this school. We do not guarantee admission and, in line with the Delhi Directorate of Education's March 2025 circular, we do not charge any donation, capitation or “facilitation” fee tied to an admission outcome. Fee figures below are dated and sourced as shown — always verify current fees and criteria directly with the school and with the DoE at edudel.nic.in.

Why admission here is considered difficult

St. Columba's combines an 84-year legacy, a boys-only intake, a prime central-Delhi location and a long list of distinguished alumni — the school itself is widely reported to carry "many names on the waiting list." Selection otherwise follows the standard DoE process.
