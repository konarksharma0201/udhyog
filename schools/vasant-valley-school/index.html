Vasant Valley School — Admission, Fees & Ranking Guide

Quick answer: Vasant Valley School (Sector C, Vasant Kunj) is a CBSE day school founded in 1990 by the India Today/Education Today group, on an 8-acre campus with a roughly 11:1 student-teacher ratio. The EducationWorld India School Rankings 2025-26 placed it in the newly introduced 'Ivy League India' five-star category.

Independent information page. Udyog Growth is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an agent of this school. We do not guarantee admission and, in line with the Delhi Directorate of Education's March 2025 circular, we do not charge any donation, capitation or “facilitation” fee tied to an admission outcome. Why admission here is considered difficult

Vasant Valley carries one of Delhi's strongest day-school reputations, reinforced by the EducationWorld India School Rankings 2025-26 (based on a nationwide parent/expert survey across 34 cities) placing it in the newly introduced "Ivy League India" five-star category. Combined with a relatively small, campus-constrained intake, this drives sustained oversubscription at nursery level. No official applicant-to-seat ratio is published by the school.

Frequently asked questions

Who founded Vasant Valley School?

Vasant Valley School was founded in 1990 by the India Today / Education Today group.

Is Vasant Valley a CBSE school?

Yes, Vasant Valley School follows the CBSE curriculum from Nursery to Class XII.

What is the fee of Vasant Valley School?

Precise, school-confirmed figures are not publicly available; aggregator estimates place first-year admission costs around Rs 3.73-4.52 lakh, but always confirm current fees directly with the school.

How to get admission in Vasant Valley nursery?

Vasant Valley follows Delhi's standard DoE nursery points-and-lottery process like every other private unaided school — there is no separate entrance test at the nursery level. See our Delhi nursery admission guide for the full process.

How big is the Vasant Valley campus?

The campus is approximately 8 acres, with a student-teacher ratio of roughly 11:1.
