# Udyog Growth — Keyword Cluster Plan (v1, 4 Oct 2026)

Source: `seo/keyword-map.json` (72 pages, 17 clusters, 519 FAQ long-tail keywords, 391 H2 topics) and `seo/cluster-gaps.json` (service × city matrix, 39 gaps).

## Principles
1. One pillar per service; city spokes only where the authority, portal, threshold or fee genuinely differs (UP vs Haryana vs Delhi vs Bihar). No thin duplicates where the process is national (GeM, Udyam, IEC → serve from the pillar with city sections, not separate pages).
2. Sub-topic spokes for the questions that actually get searched (notice types, licence types, business types).
3. Every spoke links up to its pillar, sideways to its city siblings, and across to the two most adjacent services in that city (GST ↔ FSSAI ↔ Company ↔ Shop Act ↔ Trademark ↔ Income tax).
4. Win on: 2026-rule accuracy, named local authority/portal/office, local areas, an anonymised case, FAQ schema matching visible content, and a tool link where one exists.
