Shop Act, EPF, ESIC & Professional Tax Registration Patna

Quick answer: A Patna employer needs five registrations most NCR advice gets wrong for Bihar: Shop & Establishment (no 20-employee threshold here), Professional Tax (levied in Bihar, not in Delhi-NCR), ESIC at 10+ employees, EPF at 20+, and a factory licence for manufacturers. We check the thresholds, file with the Labour Resources Department, Commercial Taxes, EPFO and ESIC offices in Patna, and set wages up to the Labour Code 50% rule.

Documents required for Shop Act, EPF and ESIC registration

  • PAN of the establishment and of the proprietor/partners/directors
  • Certificate of incorporation / partnership deed / Udyam, and GST certificate if held
  • Premises proof — rent agreement or ownership document, latest electricity bill
  • Photograph of the establishment with the name board (Shop Act — many states require the board in the local language)
  • Employee list with date of joining, designation, wages and Aadhaar; employer's and authorised signatory's Aadhaar
  • Bank account details and a cancelled cheque in the establishment's name
  • Digital Signature Certificate of the authorised signatory (EPF/ESIC)
  • Date of first employment / date the headcount crossed the threshold (decides the registration date and any arrears)
  • For factories: plan approval and factory-licence documents under the OSH Code

Exact requirements vary by category and state; we confirm the list for your case before filing so the application is not returned.

Frequently asked questions

Does my Patna office need Shop & Establishment registration?

Yes — Bihar's Shops and Establishments Act applies to shops and commercial establishments in notified areas, and Bihar has not adopted the 20-employee threshold that UP and Haryana introduced in 2025. Registration is through the Bihar Labour Resources Department's online portal; we file it with the registers the inspector checks.

Does Bihar have Professional Tax?

Yes. Unlike Delhi, UP and Haryana, Bihar levies Professional Tax on employers and employees under the Bihar Tax on Professions, Trades, Callings and Employments Act, 2011, administered by the Commercial Taxes Department. A Patna employer needs the PT registration (and enrolment) alongside Shop Act, EPF and ESIC — most NCR-based advice misses it.

When do EPF and ESIC apply in Patna?

EPF at 20 or more employees (wage ceiling ₹15,000 for mandatory coverage; 12% + 12%); ESIC at 10 or more employees in notified areas (wage ceiling ₹21,000; 0.75% + 3.25%). The ₹30,000 ESIC ceiling you may have read about has not been notified. Patna is a notified ESIC area.

Which offices handle it?

EPFO Regional Office, Patna (Bhavishyanidhi Bhawan, R-Block, Road No. 6, Patna 800001; 0612-2227410) for PF establishments; ESIC Regional Office Bihar (Panchdeep Bhawan, J.L. Nehru Marg, Patna 800001; 0612-2521928); the Labour Resources Department for Shop Act and the OSH Code registrations; the Commercial Taxes Department for Professional Tax. Both offices are as listed in the EPFO and ESIC official directories. We appear at all four.

What changed with the Labour Codes for a Bihar employer?

The four Codes took effect on 21 November 2025. For a Patna establishment the practical items are the wage definition (basic + DA at least 50% of total pay), mandatory appointment letters, single registration under the OSH Code at 10+ workers, and the factory-licence threshold at 20+ workers with power. Bihar's state rules are being notified; we check the live status before filing.

Can you handle a PMFME or BIADA unit's labour registrations too?

Yes — most Bihar food-processing units we set up need Shop Act or factory licence, EPF, ESIC and Professional Tax sequenced with the FSSAI licence, BSPCB consent and the bank file. One team, one timeline.
