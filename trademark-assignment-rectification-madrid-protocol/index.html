Trademark Assignment (TM-P), Rectification (TM-O), Madrid Protocol Filing and Trademark Watch — Official Fees and Steps

Quick answer: Four trademark transactions come up after registration. Assignment — recording a new owner — is filed in Form TM-P at ₹9,000 per mark by e-filing (₹10,000 physical) under the First Schedule of the Trade Marks Rules 2017. Rectification or removal of someone else's mark, and oppositions, are filed in Form TM-O at ₹2,700 per class by e-filing (₹3,000 physical). An international filing under the Madrid Protocol starts from an Indian basic mark, pays an Indian handling fee of ₹5,000 (e-filing only) for certification in Form MM2(E), and the WIPO basic fee of 653 Swiss francs for a black-and-white mark or 903 for colour, plus fees per designated country. A watch service monitors the Trade Marks Journal so that an opposition can be filed inside the window.

Assignment (TM-P)

  • A registered mark or a pending application can be assigned with or without goodwill, in whole or for part of the goods and services; the new owner applies in Form TM-P with the assignment deed, and the Registry enters the assignee's name, the date and the basis of assignment on the register
  • Certification marks need the Registrar's consent; assignment to different people for different parts of India is restricted — draft the deed accordingly
  • The deed is stamped per the state's schedule (stamping and registration); update ownership before renewal or enforcement so notices and certificates issue in the right name

Rectification, removal and opposition (TM-O)

  • Opposition to a pending application: within three months of its advertisement in the Trade Marks Journal, extendable by one month on request
  • Rectification or removal of a registered mark: for non-use — no bona fide use for a continuous period of five years and three months — or because the registration should not have been made (earlier rights, descriptiveness, bad faith)
  • Evidence of use or of prior rights decides these matters; our objection and hearing practice covers the defending side

Trademark watch

A watch service scans the weekly Trade Marks Journal and new filings for marks similar to yours in your classes, so that an opposition is lodged inside the statutory window instead of litigating after registration. It matters most for brands that have just registered or are about to launch in new classes.

Frequently asked questions

What is the fee for trademark assignment in India?

₹9,000 per mark by e-filing in Form TM-P, or ₹10,000 if filed physically, under the First Schedule of the Trade Marks Rules 2017.

What is Form TM-O used for?

Notices of opposition and applications for rectification or removal of a mark from the register, at ₹2,700 per class by e-filing or ₹3,000 per class physically.

How long do I have to oppose a trademark application?

Three months from its advertisement in the Trade Marks Journal, extendable by one month on request.

When can a registered trademark be removed for non-use?

When it has not been used in good faith for a continuous period of five years and three months.

What does a Madrid Protocol filing cost from India?

An Indian handling fee of ₹5,000 plus WIPO's basic fee of 653 Swiss francs (black-and-white) or 903 (colour), a supplementary fee for classes beyond three, and the fee of each designated country.

Can I file a Madrid application without an Indian trademark?

No — you need a pending or registered Indian mark as the basic mark.

What is a trademark watch?

A service that monitors the Trade Marks Journal and new filings for marks similar to yours so that you can oppose in time.

Do I need to record an assignment with the Registry?

Yes — the new owner applies in Form TM-P so the register shows the assignee; until then notices and renewals go to the old owner.
