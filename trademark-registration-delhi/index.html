Trademark Registration Delhi — Search, File & Defend at the Delhi Registry

Quick answer: Delhi applications are examined and heard at the Trade Marks Registry, Delhi (IP Office, Dwarka), which also covers Haryana and UP. Government fee is ₹4,500 per class for individuals, startups and Udyam MSMEs, ₹9,000 for others. Delhi's wholesale markets generate the densest conflicts in Classes 24, 25 and 35 — we search phonetic and transliterated variants, file in the right classes, and appear at the hearing.

What is specific to Delhi: Delhi's wholesale markets generate the densest conflicts in the registry — a Karol Bagh or Chandni Chowk brand will meet dozens of similar marks in Classes 24, 25 and 35, so the clearance search has to cover phonetic and Hindi-transliterated variants, and prior-use evidence (old invoices, trade-fair catalogues) often decides a Section 11 objection. Delhi is also where the registry sits, so hearings list faster than for applicants routed to other offices.

Frequently asked questions

Which Trade Marks Registry handles Delhi applications?

The Trade Marks Registry, Delhi — at the Intellectual Property Office, Boudhik Sampada Bhawan, Sector 14, Dwarka — for applicants whose principal place of business is in Delhi, Haryana, Uttar Pradesh and the other northern states. Filing is online; examination, hearings and oppositions sit with this office.

What is the government fee for a trademark in Delhi in 2026?

₹4,500 per class e-filed for individuals, startups and Udyam-registered MSMEs; ₹9,000 for others (₹5,000 / ₹10,000 on physical filing). Expedited examination ₹20,000 / ₹40,000. Renewal ₹9,000 per class, filable up to a year before expiry.

Which classes do Delhi businesses usually need?

Chandni Chowk and Karol Bagh wholesalers: Classes 24–25 (textiles, apparel), 14 (jewellery), 35 (retail/wholesale); Nehru Place and Okhla: 9 and 42 (hardware, software), 7 and 11 (machinery); Connaught Place and Khan Market: 43 (restaurants), 3 (cosmetics), 35; educational institutes: 41. Filing in one class when you trade in two is the most expensive error.

How long does registration take?

Filing is same-day; the examination report typically comes within months; if unopposed after advertisement (4-month window), registration commonly follows within 12–18 months of filing. You can use ™ from filing and ® after registration.

Can I attend my trademark hearing in person in Delhi?

Yes — show-cause hearings for objections and opposition hearings are listed at the Delhi registry (increasingly by video). We appear; you do not need to.

Is a trademark registered in Delhi valid all over India?

Yes — registration is national. The Delhi office is only the jurisdiction that examines and hears your application; the right applies across India and is the basis for Madrid Protocol filings abroad.
