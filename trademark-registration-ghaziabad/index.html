Trademark Registration Ghaziabad — Search, File & Defend

Quick answer: Ghaziabad applications are examined by the Trade Marks Registry, Delhi (jurisdiction for Uttar Pradesh), with hearings in Delhi. Government fee is ₹4,500 per class for individuals, startups and Udyam MSMEs, ₹9,000 for others. Sahibabad, Meerut Road and Bulandshahr Road manufacturers mostly need Classes 6, 7, 9, 11 and 12 — often as device marks stamped on the product — and we file the word and the logo together where that is the brand.

What is specific to Ghaziabad: the brand on a Sahibabad Site 4 or Meerut Road product is usually a logo embossed on the item or printed on the carton — so the device mark matters as much as the word, and OEM buyers increasingly want proof of registration, not a pending application. Loni and Bulandshahr Road units supplying Delhi dealers face the same crowded registers as Delhi itself: the search has to cover phonetic and transliterated variants.

Frequently asked questions

Which registry handles trademark applications from Ghaziabad?

The Trade Marks Registry, Delhi — the office for Uttar Pradesh applicants. Filing is online; examination, objections and hearings sit with Delhi.

What is the fee for a trademark in Ghaziabad?

₹4,500 per class e-filed for individuals, startups and Udyam MSMEs; ₹9,000 for others; expedited examination ₹20,000 / ₹40,000; renewal ₹9,000.

Should a manufacturer register the logo or the brand name?

Both, if both appear on the product — the word protects the name in any style; the device protects the stamped or printed logo buyers recognise. Filing only the word leaves the embossed mark unprotected.

How long does registration take?

Filing is same-day; if unopposed, registration commonly follows within 12–18 months. ™ from filing, ® after registration.

Can prior use beat a competitor who filed first?

Often — Indian law protects prior users; a user affidavit with dated invoices, catalogues and advertisements supports both an objection reply and an opposition against the later filer.

Do OEM buyers require a registered trademark?

Many large buyers now ask for the registration certificate at vendor onboarding, not just an application number — another reason to file before the first supply contract.
