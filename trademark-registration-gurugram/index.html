Trademark Registration Gurugram — Search, File & Defend

Quick answer: Applications from Gurugram are examined by the Trade Marks Registry, Delhi (jurisdiction for Haryana), with hearings in Delhi. Government fee is ₹4,500 per class for individuals, startups and Udyam MSMEs. We search across word, phonetic and transliterated variants plus the MCA register, file in the right classes, and handle objections and oppositions through to registration.

Documents required to file a trademark (TM-A)

  • Applicant details — individual, firm, LLP or company; PAN and address
  • The mark — wordmark text, or a clear logo/device image (JPEG) in the exact form to be used
  • Class(es) and the goods/services specification
  • Udyam / Startup India certificate to claim the ₹4,500 individual/MSME/startup fee
  • Power of Attorney (Form TM-48) in favour of the agent/attorney
  • User affidavit with evidence (invoices, advertisements, website archives) if claiming use prior to the filing date
  • Priority document if claiming convention priority
  • For objection replies: evidence of use, dictionary/trade evidence, consent or coexistence letters where applicable

Exact requirements vary by category and state; we confirm the list for your case before filing so the application is not returned.

Frequently asked questions

Which Trade Marks Registry handles applications from Gurugram?

The Trade Marks Registry, Delhi (jurisdiction for Haryana), decided by the applicant's principal place of business. Filing is online nationally, but examination, objections and hearings sit with that office — hearings for Gurugram applicants are in Delhi.

Which classes do Gurugram businesses usually need?

It depends on the goods or services, but the typical Gurugram profile is D2C consumer brands — Class 3 (cosmetics), Class 5 (nutraceuticals), Classes 29–30 (packaged food), Class 25 (apparel) — alongside Cyber City service marks in Classes 35, 36 and 42. Filing in the wrong or an incomplete set of classes is the most expensive mistake — a new application is needed to add a class later.

What does trademark registration cost?

Government fee ₹4,500 per class for individuals, startups and Udyam-registered MSMEs filing online, ₹9,000 per class for others. A registration lasts 10 years and is renewable indefinitely. Our professional fee is quoted after the search, before filing.

How long does it take?

Filing to examination report typically runs a few months; if no objection or opposition arises, registration commonly follows within 12 to 18 months of filing. You can use the ™ symbol from filing and ® only after registration.

What happens if I get an objection?

An examination report under Section 9 (descriptiveness) or Section 11 (similarity) must be answered within 30 days; a hearing may follow. Most objections are answerable with a properly argued reply and evidence — abandonment usually comes from missing the deadline, not from the merits.

Can I file before incorporating my company?

Yes — an individual or proprietor can apply, and the mark can be assigned to the company later. If incorporation is imminent, filing in the company's name avoids an assignment step; we sequence it with the incorporation.
