Trademark Renewal, Surcharge & Restoration (TM-R) — and the Class 1–45 Guide for Delhi-NCR Trades

Quick answer: A registered trademark lasts ten years and is renewed for ten more by filing Form TM-R, which can be done up to one year before expiry: ₹9,000 per class by e-filing (₹10,000 physical). Miss the date and Section 25(3) gives six months to renew with a ₹4,500 surcharge (₹13,500 total); from six to twelve months after expiry the mark is removed and must be restored under Section 25(4) with a further ₹9,000 (₹18,000 total), at the Registrar's discretion. The Registry's reminder is Form RG-3, sent not more than six months before expiry. There is no 'TM-10' any more — pages still saying so are quoting the 2002 Rules.

  • Reminder: the Registry sends Form RG-3 to the address for service not more than six months before expiry (Rule 58) — a missed notice is not a defence, so we calendar every mark
  • Late registration cases: where registration itself came within six months before or after the renewal date, renewal is allowed within six months of the actual registration (Rule 58(2)–(3))
  • Change of owner or address first: record assignments (TM-P) and address changes (TM-M) before renewing, or the renewal issues in the wrong name
Stale terminology to ignore: "TM-10" and "TM-12" were Trade Marks Rules 2002 forms; since 2017 every renewal, surcharge and restoration request is TM-R. Pages that call the reminder "O-3" are also using the old nomenclature — it is RG-3.

Frequently asked questions

What is the trademark renewal fee in India?

₹9,000 per class by e-filing (₹10,000 physical) on Form TM-R, under the First Schedule to the Trade Marks Rules 2017.

How early can I renew?

Up to one year before the ten-year registration expires (Rule 57).

What happens if I miss the expiry date?

You have six months to renew with a surcharge of ₹4,500 (₹13,500 total per class, e-filing) under the proviso to Section 25(3); the mark stays on the register during that window.

Can an expired trademark be restored?

Yes — from six to twelve months after expiry, under Section 25(4), by TM-R with the renewal fee plus ₹9,000 (₹18,000 total, e-filing), at the Registrar's discretion; after twelve months only a fresh application remains.

Does the Registry remind me?

It sends Form RG-3 to the address for service not more than six months before expiry; failure to receive it does not extend the deadline.

Is TM-10 still used for renewal?

No. TM-10 was a 2002-Rules form; since 2017 renewal, surcharge and restoration all go on Form TM-R.

Is the renewal fee per class?

Yes — each class on the registration is renewed and charged separately.

Which class does a clothing brand or a restaurant need?

Clothing: Class 25 (goods) and usually 35 for retail/online sales; restaurant or cloud kitchen: Class 43, with 29/30 if you sell packaged food.
